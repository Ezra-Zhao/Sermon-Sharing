## Content

**The meaning of salvation — what does it mean to be saved?** A person who repents and believes can receive grace and be saved. "For this gospel is the power of God to salvation for everyone who believes." But what exactly does it mean to be saved? The Bible's use of this word is very broad. From the several meanings below, we can understand its teaching.

**To be saved has the meaning of escaping from danger.** "And when we were saved, then we found out that the island was called Malta." The "saved" spoken of here refers to escaping from danger — it has the meaning of leaving danger and entering into safety. For a person who believes in Jesus, his being saved is precisely his entering from danger into safety. The Lord Jesus once said: "…neither shall anyone snatch them out of My hand." What safety this is!

**To be saved has the meaning of escaping death.** "He has saved us from so great a death." That death can be escaped — what salvation this is! Whatever the original meaning of this verse of Scripture may be, this condition is already enough to explain the salvation that all who receive grace today obtain by believing in Jesus. We all originally should have received God's righteous judgment, but now, through the Lord's rescue, we are declared: "There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus."

**To be saved has the meaning of resurrection.** ("…knowing that He who raised up the Lord Jesus will also raise us up with Jesus, and will present us with you.") "Even when we were dead in trespasses, [He] made us alive together with Christ." According to the Bible, the descendants of Adam are all dead people who were born. To be saved means to be revived from death. Originally we were dead toward God and alive toward sin; now, having been saved through Christ's salvation, we are dead toward sin and alive toward God.

**To be saved has the meaning of being bought back.** "…the church [of God] which He purchased with His own blood" — also rendered "redemption." Christ died for us; that is, He bought us back with His precious blood. "Bought back" is also translated "redeemed." He also redeemed us from the curse. And a person who is saved has been delivered out from under the power of darkness and from futile conduct received by tradition! We are no longer held by the yoke of slavery, because Christ has set us free.

**To be saved has the meaning of reconciliation.** "For if when we were enemies we were reconciled to God through the death of His Son, much more, having been reconciled, we shall be saved by His life." Because of Adam's disobedience, we all became God's enemies, with only punishment and judgment awaiting us. Salvation means that Christ came to take our place, so that we who were at enmity with God can be reconciled to God, and we who were separated from God can draw near to God.
