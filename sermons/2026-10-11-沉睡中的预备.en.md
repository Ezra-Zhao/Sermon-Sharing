## (00:00 - 08:00) Opening · Scripture Reading · Prayer

Brothers and sisters, peace to you all! Yesterday we preached on Genesis 2:1-3 — God finished creating all things, and on the seventh day He rested from all His work.

Today we continue onward, to Genesis 2:21-23. Adam is already in the Garden of Eden. The garden is his to tend and keep, and the animals are his to name. He has everything — yet God spoke one sentence —

"**It is not good that man should be alone.**" (Gen. 2:18)

This is the first time the words "not good" appear in creation. Was the light not good? It was good. The expanse, the dry land, the sun and moon, the fish, the birds, the beasts, and man — God saw that all of it was very good. Only this one thing did God call not good.

Then God did something. What He did today established the first marriage in human history, and it also hides a great mystery. Please turn with me to Genesis chapter 2, verses 21 to 23. Let me read:

"And the LORD God caused a deep sleep to fall on Adam, and he slept; and He took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh in its place. Then the rib which the LORD God had taken from man He made into a woman, and He brought her to the man. And Adam said: 'This is now bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh; she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man.'" (Gen. 2:21-23)

[Pause for 3 seconds]

Three verses, three actions: God caused him to fall into a deep sleep, God took out a rib and fashioned it into a woman, and God brought her to him.

Today we will look at this in three parts:

First, why did God cause him to fall into a deep sleep — preparation is at work while you are sleeping;
Second, the mystery of the rib — not from the head, not from the foot, but from beside the rib;
Third, God personally "brought" her to him — God is the first matchmaker.

Let us first bow our heads in prayer:

"Heavenly Father, we thank You! You saw Adam alone, and You said it is not good that man should be alone. Today You also see the loneliness, the waiting, and the struggles of every one of us. Open our eyes, we pray, to see that Your preparing hand has never stopped; open our hearts to hear You say to us: Child, you sleep — I will do the work. In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ we pray, Amen!"

## (08:00 - 24:00) Part One: Why Did God Cause Him to Fall into a Deep Sleep?

### 1.1 What Does "Deep Sleep" Mean in the Original Text?

Verse 21: "And the LORD God caused a deep sleep to fall on Adam, and he slept."

The Hebrew word for "deep sleep" is **Tardemah** — not a nap, not a doze, but a deep, completely unconscious sleep. The same word appears in Genesis 15: when God made a covenant with Abram, "as the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram" (Gen. 15:12) — a covenant was being made, Abram was asleep, and God Himself walked between the pieces of the sacrifice.

Brothers and sisters, have you noticed? In the Bible, whenever God is about to perform major surgery or establish a great covenant, man is asleep. Why?

Because **there is no part in this for you to put your hand to**. If Adam had been awake, what would he have done? He would have directed the operation: God, take this rib, not that one — gently, it hurts! God says: You sleep. I will do this; with you awake, you would only make a mess of it.

English versions for comparison:
- KJV: "And the LORD God caused a **deep sleep** to fall upon Adam, and he slept."
- NIV: "So the LORD God caused the man to fall into a **deep sleep**."
- NRSV: "So the LORD God caused a **deep sleep** to fall upon the man, and he slept."
- Lü Zhenzhong Translation: "Then the Eternal LORD God caused the man to fall into a deep sleep; and he slept."

### 1.2 Grace Is at Work While You Are Sleeping

This is a principle that runs through the whole Bible: **God's preparation takes place in man's rest.**

Psalm 127:2 says: "For so He gives His beloved sleep." The Lü Zhenzhong Translation puts it even more directly: "He will cause the one He loves to obtain while sleeping soundly." What does that mean? By day you labor; by night you entrust. God has no need of your staying up late.

How many of us today live it backwards: when it is time to sleep, we do not sleep. We lie in bed tossing and turning, thinking about work, thinking about the children, thinking about marriage, thinking about the bills. Adam fell asleep, and when he woke up he had gained a wife; we cannot sleep, and when we wake up we have gained dark circles under our eyes.

Brothers and sisters, hear this clearly: **This is not telling you to lie flat and do nothing; it is telling you to distinguish clearly which part is yours and which part is God's.** Tending and keeping the garden was Adam's work; fashioning a spouse was God's work. If you snatch God's work and do it yourself, you will work yourself into insomnia, into anxiety, into daily quarrels in your marriage — because you are doing what only God can do.

The conclusion of Part One, in one sentence: **You be responsible for faithfulness, and God will be responsible for preparation; you be responsible for sleeping in peace, and God will be responsible for doing the work.**

## (24:00 - 40:00) Part Two: The Mystery of the Rib

### 2.1 From Where It Was Taken, and From Where It Was Not Taken

Verse 22: "Then the rib which the LORD God had taken from man He made into a woman."

The Hebrew word for "rib" is **Tsela** — rib, side, the side of the body.

More than three hundred years ago, an expositor named Matthew Henry wrote a passage in his Bible commentary that has been passed down for three hundred years ever since. He said:

"The woman was made of a rib out of the side of Adam: not made out of his head to rule over him, nor out of his feet to be trampled upon by him, but out of his side to be equal with him, under his arm to be protected, and near his heart to be beloved."

(Source: Matthew Henry, *Commentary on the Whole Bible*, on Genesis 2:22; the Chinese is a rendered translation)

Brothers and sisters, why has this passage been passed down for three hundred years? Because it lays out the order of marriage in one stroke: **not ruling over, not trampling upon, but equality, protection, and love.**

Husbands, today God asks you: Is your wife above your head, or under your feet? If you trample her under your feet, this sermon today is preached for you; if you are ruled over by her until you can hardly breathe, this sermon today is also preached for you. Return to the position beside the rib — equality, protection, and love. That is God's design.

### 2.2 The Wound in the Side Points to the Cross

The rib in the Old Testament has an echo in the New Testament. John 19:34: "But one of the soldiers pierced His side with a spear, and immediately blood and water came out."

Adam's side was opened, and out came a woman; Christ's side was pierced, and out flowed blood and water — and what came out was the church. In Ephesians 5, the apostle Paul directly connects these two things:

"'For this reason a man shall leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh.' This is a great mystery, but I speak concerning Christ and the church." (Eph. 5:31-32)

Brothers and sisters, your marriage is not merely a marriage certificate and a wedding ceremony. Paul says this is "a great mystery" — earthly marriage is a rehearsal and a witness of the heavenly love between Christ and the church.

Therefore a husband is to love his wife "just as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself for her" (Eph. 5:25). To what extent did He give Himself? To the extent that His side was pierced and blood and water flowed out. Brothers, once this standard is set before us, who among us dares to say he has attained it? This is why marriage needs the gospel — because only a person filled with the love of Christ can give a self-giving love.

### 2.3 "Bone of My Bones and Flesh of My Flesh"

In verse 23, Adam wakes up, sees Eve, and speaks the first words of love in human history:

"This is now bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh."

In Hebrew, "man" is **Ish**, and "woman" is **Ishshah** — the same root, one masculine, one feminine. The moment Adam opens his mouth, he is declaring: She and I are of the same source, the same substance!

"She shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man." — To name is authority, and it is also claiming. Adam named the animals — that was management; Adam named Eve — that was claiming: She is mine, and I am hers.

Proverbs 18:22 says: "He who finds a wife finds a good thing, and obtains favor from the LORD." The Lü Zhenzhong Translation: "He who finds a good wife finds a blessing, and obtains favor from the Eternal LORD." Note this — a good wife is "given by the LORD" (Prov. 19:14); she is not someone you chased down, not someone you schemed to get; she is the one God fashioned for you while you were in deep sleep.

## (40:00 - 50:00) Part Three: God Personally "Brought" Her to Him

### 3.1 God Is the First Matchmaker

In verse 22 there are also these words, which are very easy to slip past: "**and He brought her to the man**."

After God fashioned Eve, He did not simply place her somewhere in the garden and say, "Adam, go and find her yourself." With His own hand God brought her to Adam. Brothers and sisters, this is the first wedding in the Bible — the officiant was God Himself, the bridegroom had slept a sleep, and the bride was fashioned by God's own hand.

In your marriage today, did God take part? Did you find this person yourself, chase this person yourself, decide on this person yourself — or did God bring this person to you?

I am not saying that matchmaking is unspiritual, or that free dating is unspiritual. I am saying: **No matter how you came to know each other, the key is whether this is the person God brought to you.** The one God brings, God takes responsibility for; the one you snatch for yourself, you take responsibility for to the very end.

### 3.2 Preparation, Presentation, Naming — The Three Steps of God's Work

Look at the rhythm of God's work:

Step one, **preparation**: Adam is asleep, and God is at work. When you cannot see it, God is not idle.
Step two, **presentation**: God brought her to him. When the time came, God set the one He had prepared right before you.
Step three, **naming**: Adam said, "This is now bone of my bones." Man must open his mouth to claim her, and open his mouth to enter into covenant.

How many people are stuck between step one and step two: God is still preparing, and you have already lost patience and started doing it yourself. King Saul is an example — Samuel had not come, he could not wait any longer, he offered the sacrifice himself, and as a result he lost the kingdom (1 Sam. 13:8-14). How many tragedies in marriage are written by those three words: "could not wait."

Brothers and sisters, today God says to you: **When the time has not yet come, you sleep; when the time has come, I will bring her to you.**

## (50:00 - 60:00) Application for Six Groups of People · Altar Call · Response Prayer

### Application One: For Seekers Who Have Not Yet Believed in the Lord

Friend, as you sit here today, this may be the first time you have heard about Adam and Eve. But you have certainly experienced loneliness — "It is not good that man should be alone" — this sentence was written for all mankind. That emptiness in your heart cannot be filled by education, cannot be filled by salary, and cannot be filled by romance either.

Today, through Adam's story, God tells you: He sees your loneliness, and He has a preparation. He sent His Son Jesus, whose side was pierced, from which blood and water flowed out — to open a way for you. Today, are you willing to know this God who has prepared everything for you?

### Application Two: For Single Brothers and Sisters Who Are Waiting for Marriage

Brothers and sisters, are you praying on the one hand, "Lord, prepare one for me," while on the other hand scrolling through dating apps every night until two in the morning?

Today God says: You sleep. This does not mean you should not go on introductions or not socialize; it means you should hand over your anxiety. God is at work in the place you cannot see, and His preparation is never late. You just be faithful — faithful in your work, faithful in your service, faithful in your growth. When the time comes, God will bring that person to you.

Remember Proverbs 19:14: "A prudent wife is from the LORD." She is given, not snatched.

### Application Three: For Couples Who Are Quarreling and Hurting in Marriage

When a husband and wife quarrel, by the end they are often not quarreling about that matter at all; they are quarreling about position — What right do you have to control me? What right do you have not to listen to me?

Return to today's Scripture: not from the head, not from the foot, but from beside the rib. Husband, your wife is not your subordinate, nor is she your superior; she is the person beside your rib — equality, protection, and love. Wife, your husband is not your enemy either; he is the person who is "bone of your bones and flesh of your flesh" with you.

When you go home today, do one thing: say one sentence to the other person — "You are bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh." Do not look down on this sentence. Adam spoke it three thousand years ago; today let us pick it back up.

### Application Four: For New Believers Who Are Learning to "Trust"

You may have only recently believed in the Lord, and you are still learning how to pray and how to trust. This sermon today gives you one sentence: **When God is at work, you are very often asleep.**

Do not assume that God is doing nothing just because you cannot see it. While Adam was asleep, the first marriage in human history was taking shape. Just because you cannot see it now does not mean God is not at work. To trust is to be able to sleep.

### Application Five: For Parents and Small Group Leaders Who Serve

You are raising children, leading a small group, leading new believers — do you often feel, "I have to do something"?

Learn from the way God led Adam: What is yours to do, do it faithfully (tending and keeping was Adam's); what is not yours to snatch, entrust it (fashioning Eve was God's). You cannot change your child, and you cannot change your group members — what you can do is water faithfully, sleep and entrust, and God will give the growth (see 1 Cor. 3:6-7).

### Application Six: For the Whole Church — Returning to the Great Mystery

Finally, return to Ephesians 5:32: "This is a great mystery, but I speak concerning Christ and the church."

The church is that "woman" who came out of Christ's side. Every time we gather, every time we worship, every time we take the Lord's Supper, we are re-enacting that scene: the Bridegroom laid down His life for us, and the bride is brought before Him.

So, church, stop treating yourself like an orphan nobody wants. You are the "bone of His bones and flesh of His flesh," fashioned by God's own hand and brought by God's own hand before Christ.

### 🔥 Ignition Moment

[Music begins, lights dim]

Brothers and sisters, I want to ask you three questions. You do not need to answer me; answer the God in your heart.

The first question: **Right now, are you snatching God's work?** The very part where God is to do the work — you lose sleep over it every day, you are anxious over it every day, you do it yourself every day. Today, God says: Child, you sleep — I will do it.

The second question: **That person beside you — where have you placed them?** Above your head? Under your feet? Or beside your rib? Today, God says: Return to beside the rib — equality, protection, and love.

The third question is for friends who have not yet given their lives to Jesus: **Adam slept a sleep, and when he woke up he received a wife; today, are you willing to "sleep" in Christ, and wake up to receive eternal life?** Christ's side has already been pierced for you, and His blood and water have already flowed out for you. He is waiting for you to nod.

[Pause for 5 seconds]

It is today, not tomorrow. God's preparation is never late; do not let your response be late either.

### Altar Call

Now I will make three calls:

**The first call**: Single brothers and sisters, are you willing to hand over your anxiety about marriage, and say to God, "Lord, I will sleep; You do the work"? If you are willing, please stand up.

**The second call**: Couples who have come together, are you willing to return to the position beside the rib, and say to each other, "You are bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh"? If you are willing, husband and wife, please take each other by the hand.

**The third call**: Friends who have not yet received Jesus, are you willing today to receive this Lord whose side was pierced for you? If you are willing, please raise your hand, and I will pray for you.

[Wait for responses; pray for those who raised their hands]

### Closing Response Prayer

Let us bow our heads together in prayer:

"Heavenly Father, we thank You! You caused Adam to fall into a deep sleep, and while he was sleeping, You fashioned for him bone of his bones and flesh of his flesh. Today You also say to us: You sleep — I will do the work.

Lord, we give back to You the work we snatched — the anxiety about marriage, the future of our children, the burdens of our work, we entrust them all to You. We learn to sleep in peace, because we know that You never sleep.

Lord, we also pray for married couples: bring them back to the position beside the rib — equality, protection, and love. Let every couple say again: This is now bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh.

Lord, we pray for our single brothers and sisters: may they be faithful in the waiting, and wait in rest. When the time comes, You Yourself will bring that person to them.

Lord, we pray for the friends who made a decision today: the blood and water from Your side have already flowed out for them. Please bring them Yourself before You, and make them part of Your bride.

Thank You. You are the God who causes man to fall into deep sleep, and even more, the God who prepares for man. In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ we pray, Amen!"
