## Content

### II. The Problem of Death

1. The origin of death (Rom. 6:23) (1 Cor. 15:56) (James 1:15)
2. The spread of death (Rom. 5:12) (Eccl. 9:5)
3. The reign of death (Rom. 5:14) (Eccl. 8:8)
4. The universality of death (1 Cor. 15:22) (Rom. 5:15)
5. The appointment of death (Heb. 9:27) (Eccl. 7:2)

Just as death is an unavoidable experience, so sin is a fact that cannot be concealed; just as sin is a nature that cannot be changed, so death is an inevitable result. (Ezek. 18:20)

### III. The Problem of Satan

[Scripture] (Isa. 14:12–15) (Ezek. 28:11–19)

1. He rules the world (1 John 5:19) (John 12:31)
2. He deceives the whole world (Rev. 12:9) (2 Cor. 11:13–14) (2 John 7) (Mark 14:38)
3. He manipulates the hearts of men (Eph. 2:2) (Acts 5:3)

Fallen mankind, because Satan has become the ruler of this world, has fallen under his power — manipulated, deceived, benighted all his life, until death ends it all.

God — the Law — Sin — Death — Satan. Under the fundamental problems, man's miserable condition: the captured Samson (Judg. 16:21) — the captured, the oppressed, the blind.

### Homework Questions

1. What are the fundamental problems of human life?
2. We originally paid no attention to these problems; why do we now feel they are urgent?
3. Are you a sinner? Why?
4. Is it because a man sins that he is a sinner, or because he is a sinner that he surely sins?
5. Where did death come from? If Adam had not sinned, would he have died?
6. Is Satan merely a synonym for evil, or is he a living being?
7. Besides opposing God, what else does he do in this world?
