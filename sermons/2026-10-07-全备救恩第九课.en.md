## Content

### II. Christ Died

1. Slain as a sacrifice (1 Cor. 5:7) (Isa. 53:10)
2. Died for sinners (Rom. 5:6, 8) (2 Cor. 5:14–15)
3. Died for my sins (1 Cor. 15:3) (Isa. 53:4–5)
4. Tasted death (Heb. 2:9) (Acts 2:23–24) (2 Tim. 1:10)

Christ came, and Christ also died. "Who Himself bore our sins in His own body on the tree" (1 Pet. 2:24) — that is, "the just for the unjust" (1 Pet. 3:18), so that "there is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus" (Rom. 8:1).

### III. Christ Lives

1. The tomb is empty (Luke 24:2–6) (Matt. 28:5–7)
2. He causes men to be born again (1 Pet. 1:3) (1 Pet. 1:23)
3. He causes men to be justified (Rom. 4:25) (Rom. 10:9–10) (John 16:10)
4. He saves to the uttermost (Heb. 7:25) (Rom. 8:34)

The living Christ is our example (Rom. 8:29); the Christ who died is our redemption (1 Cor. 1:30); the Christ who died and rose again is our life (Col. 3:4). Through His death and resurrection He causes us to be justified and also causes us to be born again; and through His intercession at the right hand of the Father, He thus saves us forever — this is the gospel of salvation!

### Homework Questions

1. Since we cannot save ourselves, who is our Savior?
2. What has the Lord Jesus Christ done for us?
3. What did He accomplish for us by His "coming"?
4. He died — for whom did He die?
5. What is the meaning of His dying for us?
6. What does Christ's resurrection have to do with us?
7. Do you believe that Christ's birth, death, and resurrection were all for you?

## Christ Has Risen! The Core of Christ's Gospel

**Introduction: If Christ is not risen.** [1 Cor. 15:14–19] "And if Christ is not risen, then our preaching is empty and your faith is also empty. Yes, and we are found false witnesses of God, because we have testified of God that He raised up Christ, whom He did not raise up — if in fact the dead do not rise. For if the dead do not rise, then Christ is not risen. And if Christ is not risen, your faith is futile; you are still in your sins! … If in this life only we have hope in Christ, we are of all men the most pitiable."

### I. Christ Truly Rose Again!

1. Physical evidence: the empty tomb. [Luke 24:5–6] …the two men said to them, "Why do you seek the living among the dead? He is not here, but is risen!…"
2. Human witnesses: all the apostles. [1 Cor. 15:5–8] "…and that He was seen by Cephas, then by the twelve. After that He was seen by James, then by all the apostles. Then last of all He was seen by me also, as by one born out of due time."

### II. It Was God Who Raised Him

[Acts 2:23–24] "Him, being delivered by the determined purpose and foreknowledge of God, you have taken by lawless hands, have crucified, and put to death; whom God raised up, having loosed the pains of death, because it was not possible that He should be held by it."

[Acts 5:30] "The God of our fathers raised up Jesus whom you murdered by hanging on a tree."

[Acts 13:33] "God has fulfilled this for us their children, in that He has raised up Jesus…"

### III. The Meaning of Christ's Resurrection

1. A trustworthy proof. [Acts 17:31] "…because He has appointed a day on which He will judge the world in righteousness by the Man whom He has ordained. He has given assurance of this to all by raising Him from the dead."
2. He has begotten us again. [1 Pet. 1:3] "Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who according to His abundant mercy has begotten us again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead."
3. He causes us to be justified. [Rom. 4:25] "[Jesus] was delivered up because of our offenses, and was raised because of our justification" (or, "Jesus was delivered up for our offenses, and was raised for our justification").
4. He poured out the Holy Spirit. [Acts 2:32–33] "This Jesus God has raised up, of which we are all witnesses. Therefore being exalted to the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, He poured out this which you now see and hear."
5. All will also be raised. [1 Cor. 15:21] "For since by man came death, by Man also came the resurrection of the dead. For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ all shall be made alive."

### IV. Bearing Witness to This

[Acts 1:8] "But you shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you; and you shall be witnesses to Me in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth."

[Acts 2:32] "This Jesus God has raised up, of which we are all witnesses."

[Acts 3:15] "…and killed the Prince of life, whom God raised from the dead, of which we are witnesses."

[Acts 5:32] "And we are His witnesses to these things, and so also is the Holy Spirit whom God has given to those who obey Him."

### V. Paul's Testimony

[Acts 17:2–3] "Then Paul, as his custom was, went in to them, and for three Sabbaths reasoned with them from the Scriptures, explaining and demonstrating that the Christ had to suffer and rise again from the dead, and saying, 'This Jesus whom I preach to you is the Christ.'"

[Acts 26:22–23] "Therefore, having obtained help from God, to this day I stand, witnessing both to small and great, saying no other things than those which the prophets and Moses said would come — that the Christ would suffer, that He would be the first to rise from the dead, and would proclaim light to the Jewish people and to the Gentiles."

This should also become the core of our testimony today!

**Conclusion:** Because He rose again, I receive a hundredfold in this age, eternal life in the age to come, an inheritance in eternity, and glory for all ages! I have obtained the resurrection life of Christ, have become a new creation, and have a living hope, waiting to receive the adoption as sons — the redemption of the body. Precisely because of this hope, and deeply knowing and being convinced that the risen Lord Christ Jesus is reigning, I can endure with patience and spend the remaining time on earth. Keep yourself holy, without spot and blameless, and meet the Lord in peace.

**The true meaning of salvation (diagram, in the original):** Born a sinner (Ps. 51:5); under the law (Rom. 3:19); transgression of the law is sin (1 John 3:4); under a curse (Gal. 3:10, 12–13); the old man crucified (Rom. 6:6–7); the record of sins; justified (Rom. 3:21, 24–26); the blood (1 John 1:7); reconciled to God (Rom. 5:10); drawing near to God (Heb. 10:19); delivered up beforehand (1 Pet. 1:20); obedience unto death (Phil. 2:8); in three days, resurrection; a new creation (2 Cor. 5:17); even one point of the law broken (James 2:10); born under the law to redeem those under the law (Gal. 4:4–5); without blemish (Heb. 4:15); He became obedient (Rom. 5:19).
