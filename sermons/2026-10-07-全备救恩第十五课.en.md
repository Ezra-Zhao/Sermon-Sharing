## Content

**What blessings does being saved bring?** Salvation is an extremely great blessing, one we cannot estimate with our own imagination. "Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ." Strictly speaking, the blessings enjoyed by a person who is saved do not refer to the worldly, earthly blessings, but to the heavenly, spiritual blessings! The former are visible; the latter are invisible. "The things which are seen are temporary, but the things which are not seen are eternal."

**Becoming God's children.** *The promise of becoming children:* "…you shall be My sons and daughters, says the LORD…" (in the original: "Father, you shall be…"). *The way of becoming children:* "But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become children of God, to those who believe in His name." *The evidence of becoming children:* "The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God." The psalmist said: "What is man that You are mindful of him, and the son of man that You visit him?" We are but dust. "Behold what manner of love the Father has bestowed on us, that we should be called children of God!" What a great blessing this is! (Matt. — as cited in the original)

**Becoming God's people.** *The prophecy of becoming His people:* "…those who were not My people I will call My people." *The fulfillment of becoming His people:* "But you are a chosen generation… God's own special people." *The position of becoming His people:* "Once you were not a people, but now you are the people of God." "People" means the populace. This blessing is not only becoming God's children, but also becoming God's people. Children belong to the family; people belong to the kingdom. This means: He has delivered us from the power of darkness and conveyed us into the kingdom of the Son of His love. We are not only people of God's household; we are also fellow citizens with the saints — citizens of heaven.

**Becoming God's heirs.** *The hope of becoming heirs:* "…that having been justified by His grace we might become heirs…" *The basis of becoming heirs:* "…and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ." *The right of becoming heirs:* "…heirs of God and joint heirs with Christ…" (1 Cor. — as cited in the original). "Heir" means an inheritor, a lawful recipient. "Children" refers to those who inherit life; "heirs" refers to those who inherit the inheritance. Thank God: in the blessings of salvation, we have not only received His life and become His children; we have also received His inheritance and become His heirs. Scripture says: "All things are yours." What a blessing this is!

### Homework Questions

1. What is the difference between this blessing and the blessings of the world?
2. Who can become God's children?
3. Is there evidence of becoming God's children?
4. What is the difference between God's people and God's children?
5. Are God's people still under Satan's power?
6. What does "God's heirs" mean?
7. What shall we inherit?
