## Content

### II. God's Arrangement Through the Ages

1. Christ in the types (Col. 2:16–17) (Heb. 10:1)

Before Christ had come, we already saw His shadow in the Old Testament. The goats, bulls, and calves of the she-goat offered under the law (Heb. 9:13), the Passover lamb, the bronze serpent lifted up, the rock that was struck, the manna that came down, the tabernacle in the wilderness… all of these are types of Christ, "imposed until the time of reformation" (Heb. 9:10).

2. Christ in history (Heb. 9:11–12) (Heb. 10:10–14) (1 Pet. 2:24)

Christ came according to the time God had arranged (Gal. 4:4), and died for sinners according to the appointed date (Rom. 5:6), "that He might be just and the justifier of the one who has faith in Jesus," manifesting His righteousness at the present time (Rom. 3:26).

3. The risen Christ (Rev. 1:18) (Rom. 4:25)

This is the declaration of the risen Christ's victory over the power of death. He not only tasted death for everyone (Heb. 2:9); He also destroyed for us the devil, who had the power of death (Heb. 2:14), so that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life. (John 3:16) (Rom. 10:9–10)

### III. God's Preparation in Eternity

1. To be with the Lord (John 14:3) (John 17:24) (1 Thess. 4:17)
2. To be glorified with the Lord (Rom. 8:17) (2 Cor. 4:16–18)
3. To reign with the Lord (Rev. 22:5) (Rev. 5:10) (2 Tim. 2:12)

(O Lord, blessed is the man who, for Your sake, willingly renounces all earthly things, restrains his nature, and by the power of the Holy Spirit crucifies the lusts of the flesh on the cross. Such a man can then offer pure prayer with a clean conscience; and all earthly things, whether outward or inward, having been utterly cast away, he is worthy to enter into serving You for long ages in heaven. (Ps. 113:7–8))

In the way of salvation that God Himself has arranged, all its wonder and abundance is what "eye has not seen, nor ear heard, nor have entered into the heart of man" (1 Cor. 2:9). Are you willing to receive His salvation?

### Homework Questions

1. Can we save ourselves by our own methods and abilities?
2. Then how can we be saved?
3. How do we know that God wants to save us?
4. Through what does God save us?
5. Before Christ was born, by what were people saved?
6. Besides the Lord Jesus Christ, is there any other way of salvation?
7. What is the salvation God has prepared for us for eternity?
