# Nothing New Under the Sun: Marriage

> **Preacher:** Guangyi Zhao

- **Series:** Nothing New Under the Sun (Eccl. 1:9)
- **Preacher:** Guangyi Zhao (2019, compiled and edited)
- **Theme:** The patterns, pitfalls, and way out in the area of marriage

## Outline

### Cases

My husband died several years ago. In order to commemorate him, must I refuse to consider remarrying? Response: You need not. For God has said that as long as the other party believes in the Lord, one may remarry with peace of mind. [1 Cor. 7:39] "A wife is bound by law as long as her husband lives; but if her husband dies, she is at liberty to be married to whom she wishes, only in the Lord."

Some cultures permit bigamy; what is its error? Response: Although some cultures allow bigamy, the Bible allows only monogamy. [1 Cor. 7:2] "Nevertheless, because of sexual immorality, let each man have his own wife, and let each woman have her own husband."

Why did God change the polygamy of the Old Testament to the monogamy of the New Testament? Response: God has never changed the marriage system, but from the beginning of creation established monogamy. [Gen. 2:18] "And the LORD God said, 'It is not good that man should be alone; I will make him a helper comparable to him.'" [Gen. 2:24] "Therefore a man shall leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and they shall become one flesh."

### Root Cause

People who marry only to take have unrealistic expectations of life, thinking that a spouse can satisfy the needs deep in their hearts. Wrong understanding: I have the right to expect my marriage partner to satisfy my needs. Otherwise, rather than two people reluctantly maintaining a loveless marriage, it would be better to divorce. Right understanding: God wants me to keep my marriage commitment. I am to seek from the Lord the satisfaction of the needs in my heart, and through my love and service bring the love of Christ to my partner. [Matt. 20:28] "Just as the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life a ransom for many."

### Steps to Resolution

Memorize [Eph. 5:21] "Submitting to one another in the fear of God." Read repeatedly Ephesians 5:22-33. Do not avoid problems; sit down and spend time resolving them. [Eph. 5:22] "Wives, submit to your own husbands, as to the Lord." [Eph. 5:23] "For the husband is head of the wife, as also Christ is head of the church; and He is the Savior of the body." [Eph. 5:24] "Therefore, just as the church is subject to Christ, so let the wives be to their own husbands in everything." [Eph. 5:25] "Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself for her," [Eph. 5:26] "that He might sanctify and cleanse her with the washing of water by the word," [Eph. 5:27] "that He might present her to Himself a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but that she should be holy and without blemish." [Eph. 5:28] "So husbands ought to love their own wives as their own bodies; he who loves his wife loves himself." [Eph. 5:29] "For no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, just as the Lord does the church." [Eph. 5:30] "For we are members of His body, of His flesh and of His bones." [Eph. 5:31] "'For this reason a man shall leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh.'" [Eph. 5:32] "This is a great mystery, but I speak concerning Christ and the church." [Eph. 5:33] "Nevertheless let each one of you in particular so love his own wife as himself, and let the wife see that she respects her husband."
