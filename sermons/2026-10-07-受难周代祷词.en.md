Intercessory Prayer

Heavenly Father who loves us, on this second day of Passion Week of our Savior Jesus Christ, we come before You with hearts hungering and thirsting for righteousness. Today, we lay down our physical food and drink, wholeheartedly seeking the living water that quenches life's thirst forever. Lord Jesus, when You entered Jerusalem more than two thousand years ago, You judged falsehood and cleansed the temple with kingly authority; today, we pray that You likewise enter our lives with authority.

【Scripture Recitation, Meditation, and Repentance】
Lord, with one accord we recite Your Word: "In response Jesus said to it, 'Let no one eat fruit from you ever again.' And His disciples heard it." (Mark 11:14)
Lord, this word carrying the authority of judgment pierces our hearts. Please forgive the falsehood in our lives that bears only "leaves"; forgive our barrenness of having only a religious shell without the fruit of the Holy Spirit. Lord, let our lives not be found devoid of fruit before You. Through these thirty-seven times of fasting, we pray that You remove our pretenses and cause us to bear the fruit of love, holiness, and obedience.

【Intercession for the Preacher and the Message】
Father, please open the ears of all people, so that the words of Scripture may act like a scalpel, cutting off the roots of greed and anxiety in our hearts; we also pray that the message will bring forth spiritual reconstruction, enabling every listener to receive freedom and transformation under the authority of Christ.

【Intercession by Name for the Whole Flock and Small Groups】
Lord, we intercede by name for all our loved ones participating in this fast. Please remember the price paid by every soldier and warrior. May You bestow spiritual food in the wilderness and preserve every co-worker, brother, and sister in physical health and spiritual alertness during this fast. We especially ask You to hear the prayer requests of each cell group, fellowship, and church, as well as the petitions of every household. May we experience during this fast the weak old self being "withered away from the roots," and also experience the abundant life of being "grafted into the true vine."

【Sevenfold Intercession for Core Matters】

For Individuals: Lord, cleanse us, grant us repentance and renewal, deepen our lives during this fast, and let us glorify the Lord's name in all things.

For Families: May the Holy Spirit work so that our family members confess their sins and repent, breaking down every obstacle, so that the promise of salvation for whole households may be realized in every home.

For the Church: May the Holy Spirit move with great power, enabling the church to shoulder its mission in changing times, spread the gospel widely, and become a golden lampstand.

For Pastors: Grant pastors the hearts of fathers and mothers, enabling them to serve faithfully, shepherd the flock, and be filled with spiritual strength.

For the Pulpit: Lord, preserve the pulpit to proclaim the truth alone, forbid all heresies and false teachings, and keep the flock vigilant, not shaken by secular winds.

For the Youth: Call the youth of this generation to break through practical obstacles, step onto the mission field, lay down their all, and become a blessing to this era.

For the Nation: Lord, may You reign over our nation, granting peace and stability. Please open the hearts of the people, so that the people of this land will turn to the true God.

【Intercession for Synchronized Churches】
We also especially remember the Prodigal Returns Church and Xinqing Church, who are fasting in sync with us. May fire descend upon the altars there as well; may the churches be united as one in Christ, and together experience the power of death and resurrection during Passion Week.

【Conclusion】
Lord Jesus, You are our King and You are our Lord. On this Day of Authority, we are willing to surrender the sovereignty of our lives back to You. May our fasting and prayers reach before Your throne.

In the victorious name of our Savior Jesus Christ we pray, Amen!

Sermon Manuscript: The Truth of Authority—From Withering to Reconstruction
Preacher: Teacher Guangyi Zhao
Scripture: Mark 11:12-26 (Focus: 11:21)
Background: 2026 Passion Week · Holy Monday (Day of Authority)

【Introduction: Peter's Amazement and Our Anxiety】
Peace to all brothers and sisters. Today is the second day of the fast.
Today we read Mark 11:21, where Peter cried out in amazement: "Rabbi, look! The fig tree which You cursed has withered away."

What was Peter amazed at? He was amazed at the efficiency. It was spoken just the day before, and by the next day it had "dried up from the roots." Does this not resemble us today? In this era of big data and fast pace, what we pursue is "instant feedback." Making money must be fast, becoming famous must be fast, and we even hope God will give immediate results when we pray. When Peter was shocked by this absolute authority upon seeing that tree, the Lord Jesus wanted to tell us through this tree: True authority is not meant for destruction, but to distinguish the true from the false, and to reclaim God's sovereignty amidst falsehood!

I. Authority Brings Judgment: Tearing Down the False Filters of the "Involution" Era
(Scripture: Mark 11:12-14)

【Line-by-Line Exegesis: Mark 11:12-14】

Verse 12: Now the next day, when they had come out from Bethany, He was hungry.

Exegesis: The "hunger" here demonstrates the complete humanity of Jesus. But its deeper meaning is: As the Messiah and Judge, He was looking for the offering (fruit) that belonged to Him.

Verse 13: And seeing from afar a fig tree having leaves, He went to see if perhaps He would find something on it. When He came to it, He found nothing but leaves, for it was not the season for figs.

Exegesis: This is a key difficulty. Since "it was not the season for figs," why did Jesus get angry? In Palestine, when a fig tree puts out leaves, it usually produces early fruit called "taksh" first. If there are leaves but no early fruit, it shows that the tree is already diseased and will not produce large figs later.

This tree perfectly simulated the temple of that time: flourishing on the outside (having leaves / having sacrifices), but withered on the inside (no fruit / no righteousness). This is a vivid portrait of today's "Moments-style piety"—it looks beautiful, but when the Lord draws near, it is all just filters.

Verse 14: In response Jesus said to it, "Let no one eat fruit from you ever again." And His disciples heard it.

Exegesis: Here Jesus exercised the authority of the "Creator." His word is judgment.


Jesus was hungry, and He went beneath the tree looking for fruit.
In today's Chinese society, the thing we lack least is "leaves." Look at social media today; everywhere are exquisite filters: online influencers' personas, the peaceful and quiet life portrayed on Moments, the exquisitely crafted slides in the workplace. Everyone is desperately trying to show "I am very successful," "I am very happy."

In the church, if we only have the check-in of fasting and gorgeous spiritual terminology, but lack the fruit of a transformed life, that is also just "leaves."
Why do young people today "lie flat"? Because they have seen through those "pie-in-the-sky promises" that have only leaves and no fruit! By cursing the fig tree, Jesus was declaring war against all false prosperity.

Application: Brothers and sisters, the Lord demands that we face our honest selves during this fast. When the Lord comes into your life at an unexpected time, can He find the fruit of love, integrity, and self-control? Or can He only find a bunch of pretty excuses? A tree with only leaves will inevitably wither under the Lord's authority.

II. Authority Brings Cleansing: Overturning the "Algorithms" and "Calculations" in Our Hearts
(Scripture: Mark 11:15-18)
Verse 15: Then Jesus went into the temple and began to drive out those who bought and sold in the temple, and overturned the tables of the money changers and the seats of those who sold doves.

Exegesis: The outer court of the temple was originally reserved for the Gentiles to pray, but now it was crowded with stalls. "Exchanging money" commercialized sacred worship, and "selling doves" exploited the poor.

Connecting to today's "utilitarianism": Have we also turned faith into an "exchange of benefits"? What Jesus overturned were not just tables, but the "success-theory algorithms" in our hearts.

Verse 17: Then He taught, saying to them, "Is it not written, 'My house shall be called a house of prayer for all nations'? But you have made it a 'den of thieves.' "

Exegesis: Jesus quoted Isa. 56:7 and Jer. 7:11. A "den of thieves" refers not only to stealing, but to a refuge from judgment. Religious leaders thought that as long as they offered sacrifices in the temple, their evil deeds outside would be covered up.

Fasting is not a safe haven to escape life's responsibilities. If we oppress colleagues and are heartless toward family members on the outside, yet act very spiritual when returning to church, then this place becomes our "den of thieves." The Lord's sovereignty requires that our life and faith must be consistent inside and out.


Next, Jesus entered the temple. He overturned the tables.
Why? Because the temple was filled with "business mentality."
Think about the current social mindset: everything is about monetization, everything is about returns. Big data algorithms trap people in desire and anxiety. We have actually brought this kind of "algorithm" into the temple:
"Lord, I paid my tithe this year; You must keep my company from laying me off."
"Lord, my child is taking the college entrance exam; I will fast a few more days, so You must give him extra points."

This kind of transaction is not faith; it is turning the temple into a "den of thieves"! You have treated God as an administrative office, and prayer as an exchange of resources.
Forceful Application: Jesus would not allow anyone to carry vessels through the temple. In this fast, we need to empty our hearts of those "vessels of anxiety" and "tables of comparison." He wants to restore the single function of the temple—prayer. Prayer is not a demand, but looking up to Him; it is not an exchange, but an entrustment.

III. Authority Brings Reversal, Faith, and Forgiveness: Faith and Forgiveness That Uproot
(Scripture: Mark 11:21-26)
【Line-by-Line Exegesis: Mark 11:20-26】

Verses 20-21: Now in the morning, as they passed by, they saw the fig tree dried up from the roots. And Peter, remembering, said to Him, "Rabbi, look! The fig tree which You cursed has withered away."

Exegesis: "Dried up from the roots" symbolizes the thoroughness of judgment. Peter's amazement revealed that his faith was still stuck at the stage of "watching miracles," without grasping the essence of "authority."

Verses 22-23: So Jesus answered and said to them, "Have faith in God. For assuredly, I say to you, whoever says to this mountain, 'Be removed and be cast into the sea,' and does not doubt in his heart, but believes that those things he says will be done, he will have whatever he says."

Exegesis: "This mountain" refers not only to difficulties; in the context of that time, it more likely referred to the "Temple Mount." Jesus was prophesying: If the temple loses its function of prayer, it will also be removed. Faith is not for seeking wealth, but for allowing God's will to be accomplished without hindrance.

Verse 25: "And whenever you stand praying, if you have anything against anyone, forgive him, that your Father in heaven may also forgive you your trespasses."

Exegesis: This is an astonishing turning point! Right after speaking of authority and faith, Jesus immediately speaks of forgiveness. For authority without forgiveness is violence, and faith without forgiveness is pride.

Today's society is full of hostility and cyberbullying. The true authority of a Christian lies in having the power, through the Lord, to forgive when facing injustice. For a person who refuses to forgive still has an unclean heart-temple, and God's power cannot flow through him.

Back to verse 21. Peter saw that the tree was "dried up from the roots."
What does the "root" represent? It represents the unbelief, greed, and fear in the deepest part of life.
Today's society is full of uncertainty. The pressure of unemployment, the anxiety of eldercare, the tearing apart of relationships—these are like bitter roots growing in our hearts. Jesus answered Peter in verse 22: "Have faith in God."

Reject Falsehood: Prune away those pretenses that have only leaves.
Clear Out Calculations: Overturn those utilitarian tables in the heart.
Proclaim Faith: By the Lord's authority, cause the roots of anxiety to wither away.
Live Out Forgiveness: Let the house of prayer for all nations flow with the love of Christ.

Application to Reality:
When you face injustice in the workplace, when you face online hostility, when you feel suffocated in this highly competitive environment, Jesus says: You have authority!
This authority is not to curse others, but through faith, to cause those "roots of anxiety" and "roots of hatred" to wither away in your life. Jesus went on to say, forgive. In this era where everyone brawls and contends, forgiveness is the highest order of authority. Being able to strike back is an instinct, but being able to forgive is sovereignty that comes from God!

【Conclusion: Reclaiming Spiritual Sovereignty in the Fast】
Brothers and sisters, on this second day of fasting, your stomach may be protesting and your body may be weak. But this is precisely the moment for you to reclaim sovereignty.
Please close your eyes and imagine Jesus walking into the temple of your heart. Which table does He want to overturn? Is it that table called "Worry"? Or that table called "Calculation"?

Closing Declaration (Bread of Life style):
Lord! We pray that You rebuild the altar of prayer in this place!
Let our lives no longer be empty shells with only leaves, but lives filled with the fruit of the Holy Spirit.
Lord! In Your name we declare that those habits of the old self that have entangled us for years, those roots of anxiety, shall wither away from the roots today!
In this turbulent era, let us look not at the circumstances, but only at the Savior who has all authority!
May Your authority, beginning from our hearts, rebuild this era!
Amen!

(Finally, leading the congregation in prayer:)
Lord! We ask You to perform surgery in our hearts. Those "roots of the old self" that have entangled us for so long, today in the name of the Lord we declare: be withered away from the roots! May Your authority manifest the power of death and resurrection in us who are willing to pay the price to fast!
Amen!
