## Content

The confirmation of salvation. Is there any evidence of being saved? [Original text partially illegible.] [Salvation] is accomplished — how it is not from one's own effort, so also the evidence of being saved is not based on one's own feelings. Everything that comes from [self/the flesh] is corrupt; everything that comes from [feelings] is also empty. Strictly speaking, every experience in the truth is entirely the work of the Holy Spirit. If one is not moved by the Holy Spirit, no one can say that Jesus is Lord. If one is not born of water and the Spirit, one cannot enter the kingdom of God. Our salvation is confirmed through the Holy Spirit.

The Spirit's begetting: "That which is born of the Spirit is spirit" (John 3:6, NKJV) (1 Peter). Salvation is not a modification of the old creation; salvation is the completion of a new creation. What is born of the flesh is forever flesh; only what is born of the Spirit is spirit. The flesh is temporary; only the spirit is eternal. The temporary cannot become the eternal; the temporary can only inherit the eternal. Our being born of the Spirit is rebirth; having been born again, we have eternal life. For eternal life is the result of rebirth, and rebirth is the beginning of eternal life.

The Spirit's baptism: "For by one Spirit we were all baptized into one body" (1 Cor. 12:13, NKJV). Being saved has not only an individual experience; being saved also has a corporate experience. Being saved is not only man's union with God; being saved is also all who believe being united with one another. Our being one with God in Christ is evidence of our salvation; our being one with all who believe, in Christ, is also evidence of our salvation. We not only have fellowship with God; we also have fellowship with one another. We not only love God; we also love those who are begotten of God. For through the baptism of the Holy Spirit we have become one body.

The Spirit's seal: "having believed, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise" (Eph. 1:13, NKJV). God has set apart all the saved and left on them the seal of the Holy Spirit. Although this seal cannot be seen with the physical eye, this seal is for the Lord's recognition, and it is for the day of redemption. All who have the Lord's seal belong to the Lord. When that day comes, not one of those who belong to the Lord will be lost.

The Spirit's earnest: "and has given us the Spirit in our hearts as a guarantee [earnest]" (2 Cor. 1:22; 5:5, NKJV) (the original word means "earnest/pledge") (2 Corinthians). God's giving the Holy Spirit in our hearts as an earnest is not only evidence of being saved; it is also evidence of obtaining the inheritance. You need not doubt whether you will be saved unto God; you should know that God has already saved you, and has come into you.

The Spirit's witness: "The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God" (Rom. 8:16, NKJV). The Holy Spirit's work is the power of our salvation; the Holy Spirit Himself is the assurance of our salvation. And here, the Holy Spirit also bears living and powerful witness to our position as saved. Not only does our own heart testify that we are God's children; He also proves that we are God's children. Is this not enough confirmation?

Homework questions: Through what, most importantly, is the evidence of our salvation confirmed? How does the Holy Spirit confirm that we are saved? What is the difference between rebirth and eternal life? To what does the Spirit's baptism relate? What is the Spirit's seal? What is the Spirit's earnest? Do you know that you yourself are saved?
