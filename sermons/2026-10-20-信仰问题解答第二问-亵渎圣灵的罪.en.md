# Faith Q&A Question 2: What Is the Sin of Blaspheming the Holy Spirit? Why Can It Not Be Forgiven?

> **Translation status:** Initial translation, pending review by a native-speaking Christian reviewer.
> **Note:** Translated from the Chinese sermon manuscript «亵渎圣灵的罪是什么？为什么不得赦免？».

**Original work:** Rev. Stephen Tong, *Faith Q&A*, Question 2

---

## Scripture

> "Therefore I say to you, every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven men, but the blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven men. Anyone who speaks a word against the Son of Man, it will be forgiven him; but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit, it will not be forgiven him, either in this age or in the age to come." (Matt. 12:31–32, NKJV)

> "Of how much worse punishment, do you suppose, will he be thought worthy who has trampled the Son of God underfoot, counted the blood of the covenant by which he was sanctified a common thing, and insulted the Spirit of grace?" (Heb. 10:29, NKJV)

---

## I. What Does "Blasphemy" Actually Mean?

Look at the Greek first. **"Blasphemy" in the Greek New Testament is *blasphēmeō* (βλασφημέω)**, and its root can be broken down as "to harm (*blaptō*) + speech (*phēmē*)" — to wound and attack with malicious words.

So Pastor Tong makes the point right away: **blasphemy is not ordinary disrespect; it is a deliberate, formal attack and insult aimed at a Person.** It is not muttering something behind someone's back; it is formal, public slander.

In Matthew 12:31–32, Jesus states it in the strongest terms: "Every sin and blasphemy will be forgiven men, but the blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven men." Note the words "will not be forgiven" — this is one of the rare absolutes in Scripture.

---

## II. First, Rule Out Two Wrong Ideas

Why the Holy Spirit in particular? Many people immediately go astray in two directions, and Pastor Tong blocks both roads first:

**Mistake one: supposing that the Holy Spirit is stricter and harder to deal with than the Father and the Son.**
Wrong. The Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit have **equal authority, equal glory, equal work, equal identity, and equal status**. In God's righteousness there is no such word as "more" — the Holy Spirit is not the fiercest member of the Trinity.

**Mistake two: supposing that blaspheming the Holy Spirit is unforgivable because the Holy Spirit has greater authority.**
Equally wrong. Pastor Tong gives an example from Hinduism: among the three Hindu gods, Shiva the destroyer is the greatest and the most feared. But the Christian Trinity **has no ranking of high and low**. When Jesus said "My Father is greater than I" (John 14:28), He was speaking of His submission to the Father in the station of the Son of Man, sent in the incarnation; in eternity, the three Persons are equal.

So the reason this sin is unforgivable is **not that the Holy Spirit has a worse temper**. There is another reason.

---

## III. Why the Holy Spirit in Particular? The Key Lies in "Application"

Pastor Tong brings in the Reformed doctrine of grace; this is the key to the whole piece:

**Grace has two parts:**
- **Common Grace:** the wisdom and abilities God gives to all mankind. It is no surprise when a non-Christian scientist accomplishes more than a Christian — he has received God's intelligence; he simply does not give God the glory.
- **Saving Grace:** the grace that brings a person back into a right relationship with God.

**Within saving grace, the Trinity has a division of labor:**
- **The Father prepares** salvation — planned before the foundation of the world
- **The Son accomplishes** salvation — He said "It is finished" on the cross
- **The Holy Spirit applies** salvation — He actually brings the grace to you

Hebrews 10:29 calls the Holy Spirit "**the Spirit of grace**" — His work is precisely to apply grace. **The Holy Spirit is the first Person to come into direct contact with the sinner:** before you ever come before Christ, it is the Holy Spirit who first moves you; your repentance and your new birth are both born of the Holy Spirit.

Now the problem comes into view: **if you reject and attack the very Spirit who personally delivers forgiveness to your door, how can forgiveness ever get in?**

- Reject the Holy Spirit → you cannot reach the Son
- Without going through the Son → you cannot reach the Father

So the essence of blaspheming the Holy Spirit is **rejecting, to the very end of one's life, the forgiving grace that the Holy Spirit applies**. A person rejects the grace of forgiveness in this age and carries the unforgiven sin into eternity — that is what "it will not be forgiven him, either in this age or in the age to come" means.

In one sentence: **it is not that God is unwilling to forgive; it is that the person has blocked the door of forgiveness shut for his whole life.**

---

## IV. Failing to Respond Once Does Not Count as Blaspheming the Holy Spirit

Someone may ask: at an evangelistic meeting, when the preacher gives the call, do those who do not raise their hands count as blaspheming the Holy Spirit?

Pastor Tong's answer is crisp: **No.** It is hard for a person to accept at once the grace the Holy Spirit applies, and God will still give him opportunity. That opportunity remains in force **throughout his lifetime**.

Only one situation constitutes the sin: resisting and opposing to the very end, refusing to accept for a whole lifetime. At that point, the person has "kept his sin" and carries his sin into eternity.

So do not use this passage to frighten people, and do not use it to judge others. **Blaspheming the Holy Spirit is not a slip of the tongue; it is the direction of a whole life.**

---

## V. For Us Today

1. Do not imagine the Holy Spirit as the strictest member of the Trinity — the three Persons are equal and equally honored
2. Forgiveness has one channel: the Father prepares → the Son accomplishes → the Spirit applies; do not block the last one yourself
3. Failing to respond once is not final rejection, but do not use "there is still opportunity" as an excuse for delay
4. The real danger is not saying one wrong sentence, but refusing for a lifetime to let the Holy Spirit work
