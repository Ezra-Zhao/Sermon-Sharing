## Content

Drawing on salvation. How can we enjoy the complete salvation? In God's complete salvation, we need do nothing at all; we need only come to receive, to draw upon it. We are all like a person drowning in a deep abyss: what the rescuer requires of us is not struggling, but obedience; not desperate fighting, but quiet trust. (Use what God has given to draw on what God has already accomplished!)

There are three things we ought to learn in drawing on salvation. They are what the Bible says: "And now abide faith, [hope, love], these three; but the greatest of these is love" (1 Cor. 13:13, NKJV). Let us use these three to receive God's complete salvation.

(Faith is the acceptance of acceptance — the acceptance of the fact that God has already accepted us.) 1. Faith is receiving: "But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become children of God, to those who believe in His name" (John 1:12, NKJV). 2. Faith is confessing: "because if you confess with your mouth... and believe in your heart..., you will be saved" — for "with the heart one believes unto righteousness, and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation" (Rom. 10:9-10, NKJV). 3. Faith is entrusting: "[I know whom I have believed and am persuaded] that He is able to keep what I have committed to Him" (2 Tim. 1:12, NKJV). If you understand God's way of salvation, you will know that in His salvation, apart from faith, there is nothing for us to do. Faith means accepting what God has accomplished; faith means confessing that the Lord is our Lord; faith also means handing over our whole person to the God who saves us! Through faith we draw on His salvation; through faith we are united with the Lord who saves us; through faith we take refuge under the shadow of God's wings; through faith we rest in His mighty embrace!

Love (love is the sum of the commandments (1 Tim.), the greatest commandment, the supreme law, the most excellent way (1 Cor.)). 1. Fulfilling the law completely: "Love does no harm to a neighbor; therefore love is the fulfillment of the law" (Rom. 13:10, NKJV). 2. The bond of perfection: "But above all these things put on love, which is the bond of perfection" (Col. 3:14, NKJV). 3. Covering offenses: "...fervent love for one another, for 'love will cover a multitude of sins'" (1 Pet. 4:8, NKJV). In present salvation, some things concern our growth, sanctification, and victory; none of these are accomplished by our own strength! Just as we draw on the past salvation concerning life by that sole faith, so also we complete the present salvation concerning living by the greatest love. With faith, everything is obtained; with love, everything is included. But please note: just as [faith] is the Lord's initiating (Hebrews) and the Lord's increasing, so also [love] is the Spirit's outpouring. [Original text partially illegible.] Without the Lord's mercy, we cannot even believe; likewise, without the Spirit's filling, we cannot love. Strictly speaking, this is not our love; it is God's love flowing out through us!

1. Hoping for what we do not see: "For we were saved in this hope" (Rom. 8:24, NKJV) (2 Corinthians). 2. Hope requires patience: "If we endure, we shall also reign with Him" (2 Tim. 2:12, NKJV). 3. Hope requires waiting: "in this present age [living] soberly... looking for the blessed hope" (Titus 2:12-13, NKJV). We know that the future portion of salvation has not yet been realized, and all the saints are longing [for it]. Cleansing ourselves and waiting to see the Lord is our intention; being watchful in prayer and preparing to give an account is our attitude. "Therefore, beloved, looking forward to these things, be diligent to be found by Him in peace, without spot and blameless" (2 Pet. 3:14, NKJV).

Homework questions: What does the saving Lord require of the one being saved? In drawing on salvation, which three things should we learn? What does faith mean? Do you truly believe in this way? What efficacy does love have? What is the difference between the love God requires and man's natural love? How can we obtain the future salvation?
