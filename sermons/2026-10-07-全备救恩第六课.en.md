## Content

### II. His Lovingkindness

1. He is love (1 John 4:7–8) (John 4:16) (1 John 4:10)
2. He so loved (John 3:16) (Rom. 5:6–10)
3. His great love (Eph. 2:4) (1 John 3:1) (John 13:1) (Eph. 1:4–5)

God cannot deny His own promise, and God cannot restrain His own love. Because He is "love," He cannot "not love." Love is the motive power of creation; love is also the fountainhead of salvation.

### III. His Desire

1. Giving up His only Son (Rom. 8:32) (Isa. 53:10)
2. Gaining many sons (Heb. 2:10) (Gal. 3:26)
3. Establishing the Firstborn (Rom. 8:29) (Ps. 89:27) (Heb. 1:6)

This is God's most wonderful desire in His eternal purpose. Before creation, there was only the only begotten Son in the bosom of the Father (John 1:18); through creation and redemption, God has gained many sons in eternity. The difference between before creation and within eternity is this: originally God had only the only Son; later God had many sons (Rom. 8:19); originally God had only one Son; later God had many sons.

In God's light, when we understand God's purpose, let us rest in His divine power and His working. For His own sake, He cannot but save us. Even if you do not hope to be saved, He will still save you, because you were made by Him and belong to Him (Isa. 43:1).

### Homework Questions

1. What are the three greatest reasons God wants to save us?
2. When did God promise eternal life?
3. Before man was created in ancient eternity, to whom did God promise eternal life?
4. How do we know that God is love?
5. How many sons did God have before creation?
6. Through creation and redemption, how many sons does God have in eternity?
7. Christ is God's only begotten Son who became the Firstborn; what about us?
