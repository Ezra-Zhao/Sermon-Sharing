### The Longing for Salvation

**Can man save himself?**

**When you understood the condition of life, did you long to be saved?**
A: When I understood the condition of life and knew that man cannot save himself, I greatly longed for a Savior to come and save me. As it is written: "Nor is there any who can deliver... no man can redeem his brother, nor give to God a ransom for him" (Ps. [reference illegible]).

**Can you be saved by resolving to do good? Why?**
A: I cannot be saved by resolving to do good, because sin is present with me (Rom. [reference illegible]); things turn out contrary to my wishes (Rom. [reference illegible]); resolving is useless (Rom. [reference illegible]); I cannot be saved (Ps. [reference illegible]). As it is written: "For the good that I will to do, I do not do." To say a heartbreaking word: it is not that the heart is unwilling; it is truly that there is no strength! It is as if the authority is not in my own hands: "For we know that the law is spiritual, but I am carnal, sold under sin."

**How is our doing of righteousness before God?**
A: Our doing of righteousness, before God, is also filthy (______ [reference illegible — the source gives a one-character book abbreviation that cannot be identified with certainty]); we cannot be justified (Rom. [reference illegible]); we fear God will call us to account (Ps. [reference illegible]).

**Can a person be justified by keeping the law?**
A: A person cannot be justified by keeping the law. Israel paid an extremely great price for this: "But Israel, pursuing the law of righteousness, has not attained to the law of righteousness." "I do not set aside the grace of God; for if righteousness comes through the law, then Christ died in vain" (______). Israel's failure was precisely because they wanted to establish their own righteousness by the law: "For they being ignorant of God's righteousness, and seeking to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted to the righteousness of God" (______), and therefore did not submit to God's law either.

**What about striving desperately and pursuing salvation?**
A: Striving desperately and pursuing salvation is also running in vain (Rom. [reference illegible]) and swift running is of no avail (Eccl. [reference illegible]). It is forever impossible for man, by his own methods and by his own effort, to reach the goal of salvation — because salvation is not something we accomplish ourselves! Apart from God's grace and mercy, all our desperate striving to pursue salvation is in vain.

**What about strictly keeping rules?**
A: Strictly keeping rules is useless. Man's corruption comes from the inheritance of his nature; one can never, by means of some rules and restraints, reach the goal of salvation.

**What about afflicting the body and restraining the flesh?**
A: Afflicting the body and restraining the flesh are likewise without any effect. As it is written: "These things indeed have an appearance of wisdom in self-imposed religion, false humility, and neglect of the body, but are of no value against the indulgence of the flesh."

**Do you know that God wants to save you?**

**Can we save ourselves by our own methods and abilities?**
A: We cannot save ourselves by our own methods and abilities, because as it is written: "Truly no man can redeem his brother, nor give to God a ransom for him." When under the illumination of the gospel we see the condition of mankind and our own situation, and the desolation of being unable to save ourselves, we cannot help but sigh: "What must I do to be saved?" In human history, many have longed for salvation: some out of pessimism and weariness of the world, some out of seeing through worldly vanity; therefore some have withdrawn to forests and springs to escape fame and profit, and some have practiced asceticism and afflicted the body. But how pitiful! Although these people were willing to pay a price, they did not find the door of salvation.

**Then, how can we be saved?**
A: Do not forget: God is the God who created us. Not only do we long to be saved; God also desires to save us, because the Lord Jesus said: "You did not choose Me, but I chose you."

**How do we know that God wants to save us?**
A: This is God's plan from before the creation of the world: foreknowledge (Rom. [reference illegible]) — "For whom He foreknew, He also predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son, that He might be the firstborn among many brethren"; predestination and election (Eph. [reference illegible]) — "just as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before Him"; grace given beforehand (2 Tim. [reference illegible]) — "who has saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to His own purpose and grace which was given to us in Christ Jesus before time began" (Acts [reference illegible]).

**Through what does God save us?**
A: God saves us through the redemption of Christ. Christ came according to the time arranged by God (Gal. [reference illegible]), and died for sinners according to the appointed date (Rom. [reference illegible]): "But now the righteousness of God... is revealed... that He might be just and the justifier of the one who has faith in Jesus."

A: Before Christ was born, when Christ had not yet come, we already saw His shadow in the Old Testament: the goats, bulls, and calves offered under the law (Heb. [reference illegible]) were all types of Christ, "appointed until the time of reformation."

**Besides the Lord Jesus Christ, is there any other way of salvation?**
A: Besides the Lord Jesus Christ there is no other way of salvation. "I am He who lives, and was dead, and behold, I am alive forevermore. Amen. And I have the keys of Hades and of Death" (______). **What is the salvation God has prepared for us for eternity?**
A: The salvation God has prepared for us for eternity is: to be with the Lord (John 14 [reference illegible]); to come with the Lord (Rom. 8 [reference illegible]); to have authority with the Lord (Rev. 22 [reference illegible]). ## Lesson Eleven: The Meaning of Salvation

**What does it mean to be saved?**

**Have you been saved by believing in Jesus?**
A: The Scripture says: a person who repents and believes in the Lord can receive grace and be saved, because "this gospel... is the power of God to salvation for everyone who believes."

**Could there be a person who believes in Jesus but is not saved?**
A: A person who truly believes will not fail to be saved, because on the cross the Lord has already accomplished the work of salvation. As long as we truly believe from the heart, we will surely be saved.

**Why does escaping danger carry the meaning of being saved?**
A: Escaping danger carries the meaning of entering into safety. A person who believes in Jesus is saved by passing from danger into safety. The Lord Jesus said: "And I give them eternal life, and they shall never perish; neither shall anyone snatch them out of My hand" (______). What safety this is! Therefore escaping danger carries the meaning of being saved.

**Why does escaping death carry the meaning of being saved?**
A: We all ought originally to receive God's righteous judgment, but now, through the Lord's salvation, we are declared: "There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus" (______). Therefore escaping death carries the meaning of being saved.

**Why does resurrection carry the meaning of being saved?**
A: According to the Bible, the descendants of Adam are all "dead men born" (Luke [reference illegible]). Being saved means being raised from the dead. Originally we were dead toward God and alive toward sin; now, through Christ's salvation we are saved: dead toward sin and alive toward God (______). Resurrection carries the meaning of being saved.

**Why does being bought back carry the meaning of being saved?**
A: Christ died for us; that is, with His precious blood He bought us back. "Christ has redeemed us from the curse of the law, having become a curse for us (______), for it is written: 'Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree'" (______). And a saved person has been brought out from under the power of darkness (Col. [reference illegible]) — "He has delivered us from the power of darkness and conveyed us into the kingdom of the Son of His love" — and from the futile way of life inherited from the forefathers (1 Pet. [reference illegible]): "knowing that you were not redeemed with corruptible things, like silver or gold, from your aimless conduct received by tradition from your fathers." We are no longer restrained by the yoke of slaves, because Christ has made us free (Gal. [reference illegible]): "Christ has redeemed us... Stand fast therefore in the liberty by which Christ has made us free, and do not be entangled again with a yoke of bondage." Therefore being bought back carries the meaning of being saved.

**Why does reconciliation carry the meaning of being saved?**
A: Because of Adam's disobedience, we all became God's enemies, only awaiting punishment and judgment. Salvation means that Christ came to take our place, so that we who were enemies of God may be reconciled to God, and we who were separated from God may draw near to God: "For if when we were enemies we were reconciled to God through the death of His Son, much more, having been reconciled, we shall be saved by His life" (______). Therefore reconciliation carries the meaning of being saved.

## Lesson Thirteen: The Standing of Salvation

**From where have we been saved, and to where?**

**Is there a difference between the standing of a sinner and the standing of one who has received grace?**
A: There is a difference: (1) from death into life; (2) from darkness into light; (3) from law into grace; (4) from sin into holiness.

**What does "from death into life" mean?**
A: In the standing of salvation, the first point is from death into life: a dead person becomes a living person, because God's salvation is full of power and life, making those who must die able not to die, and those already dead able to live again. This is from death into life.

**How can one pass from death into life?**
Believe, and it is done (John [reference illegible]); hear, and one lives (John [reference illegible]); look, and one lives (Num. [reference illegible]).

**What does "from darkness into light" mean?**
A: It is to be rescued from the power of darkness (Col. [reference illegible]), not only to become children of light, but also to enter God's light, so as to fellowship with the Lord and walk with God in the light. This is from darkness into light.

**What does "from law into grace" mean?**
