## Content

**How is salvation brought about?** From the Bible we can understand God's preparation and arrangement for salvation, and we can also understand the method and meaning of being saved. We need even more, in actual fact, to know how salvation is realized and how being saved is brought about. Let us trace back again to the beginning, starting from the Creator, and see how God, step by step, brought this salvation to realization, until God's plan became fact in us and God's grace became experience in us.

**God's love.** ("Show Your marvelous lovingkindness" (Ps. 17:7).) *The object of His love:* "For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son." *The manifestation of His love:* "God sent His only begotten Son into the world… In this the love of God was manifested." *The immeasurableness of His love:* "…to know the love of Christ which passes knowledge." *The wonder of His love:* "Behold what manner of love the Father has bestowed on us!" Because of His wonderful, immeasurable love, God gave His only begotten Son to be our Savior and made us God's children — this is the fountainhead of salvation! Without God's love, there would be nothing at all.

**The coming of grace.** "…grace and truth came through Jesus Christ." *The giving of grace:* "…the grace that was given us in Christ Jesus" (2 Tim. 1:9). *The fullness of grace:* "And of His fullness we have all received, and grace for grace." *Grace reigning:* "…so grace might reign through righteousness to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord." Because of God's love, there is the Lord's grace. As His love is immeasurable, so His grace is especially abundant! God's love is the motive; the Lord's grace is the supply. God's love is the purpose; the Lord's grace is the execution. Without God's love, salvation would have no fountainhead; without the Lord's grace, the way of salvation would lose its basis.

**The work of the Holy Spirit.** *Making man know sin:* "And when He has come, He will convict the world of sin…" *Leading man to acknowledge the Lord:* "…and no one can say that Jesus is Lord except by the Holy Spirit." *Making man born again:* "…unless one is born of water and the Spirit…" *Making man renewed:* "…according to His mercy He saved us, through the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Spirit." In the realization of salvation, it rests not only on God's love and passes through the Lord's grace; it is also through the work of the Holy Spirit. We should know that if there were no work of the Holy Spirit, neither God's love nor the Lord's grace would be applied to us. The Holy Spirit's work here is like that of an executor, who transports God's love and the Lord's grace into us and channels them through us, making truth become fact and making salvation realized.

### Homework Questions

1. What are the three steps by which God brought about so great a salvation?
2. Scripture says God loves the world — who is "the world"?
3. Through such love, what relationship can we have with God?
4. From where did grace come?
5. What is the difference between God's love and the Lord's grace?
6. With only God's love and the Lord's grace, but without the Spirit's work, can we be saved?
7. Why is the work of the Holy Spirit so essential?
