Many people experience healing, only to lose it; or they believe the truth of healing, yet cannot hold fast to it in their daily lives. Why?

The key to this chapter is just one verse of Scripture:

> "And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God." (Rom. 12:2)

The key to retaining healing is to be transformed by the renewing of your mind. You need to be renewed in three areas: how you see God and yourself, how you see your own body, and how you see the surrounding environment.

## 1. Renew Your Perspective on God and Yourself

### 1. God Is Good

This is the starting point and the foundation. What God has toward us and toward this world is a good, acceptable, and perfect will. He desires to see this world restored to the complete and perfect state of its original creation, and to have His kingdom invade every corner of this world.

If in your heart you feel that God is harsh, stingy, and quick to send disaster, your mind has not yet been renewed. First engrave "God is good" upon the tablet of your heart.

### 2. You Are a New Creation

"The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God, and if children, then heirs—heirs of God and joint heirs with Christ..." (Rom. 8:16-17)

Because of Jesus' redemption, you are saved, healed, and delivered. You are a new creation; old things have passed away. Your identity is in Christ: you are a child of royalty, loved, blessed, and highly favored.

"having been born again, not of corruptible seed but incorruptible, through the word of God which lives and abides forever," (1 Pet. 1:23)

In the incorruptible seed there is no death. "For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has made me free from the law of sin and death." (Rom. 8:2) We do not have to gradually march toward death, nor do we need to assume that as we age, our bodies must inevitably suffer sickness and decay.

### 3. Understand Grace

Grace is a fundamental truth, yet it is often misunderstood. In Greek lexicons, "grace" (χάρις, charis) (original text χάρις, transliteration charis; Strong's G5485; according to *Thayer's Greek Lexicon*, definition 2a: "the divine influence upon the heart, and its reflection in the life; of the merciful kindness of God, by which He, exerting His holy influence upon souls, turns them to Christ," see Strong's definition) is defined as: the divine influence upon the heart, and its reflection in one's response. Its root signifies joy, gladness, rejoicing, and celebration.

Grace is entirely about God—a Giver full of good will and benevolent character (Eph. 1). Grace is not merely "unmerited favor," but goes far beyond it (Eph. 2:4-10). Grace originates from a new and better covenant (Heb. 8:6-13).

A key sentence: The Giver delights to see the recipients of His favor **receive** grace, rather than reject or squander it (Heb. 12:15). God has already given you healing; are you willing to reach out your hand and receive it?

## 2. Renew Your Perspective on Your Body

### 1. Your Body Is the Temple of the Holy Spirit

The One who raised Jesus from the dead lives in you. "He who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through His Spirit who dwells in you." (Rom. 8:11)

From this day forward, speak these words to your body:

- Sickness and pain are not my identity; I will never accept that identity again;
- By the salvation of Jesus, I am healed;
- The lie of sickness says, "It's back again," but I do not fear because symptoms reappear;
- I possess kingdom authority over God's temple (my body);
- My focus is on what God has already accomplished for me, not on what has not yet been accomplished.

### 2. Steward God's Temple with Wisdom

You bear the primary responsibility for stewarding God's temple (your body), so make wise choices:

**Healthcare**: If you have professional healthcare providers, be sure to ask them to examine and verify the healing you have experienced. If you are taking medication, please do not stop taking it on your own without the consent of medical personnel. Medications have a significant impact on the body's chemistry, so caution is required when choosing to take or discontinue them.

**Dietary Habits**: Choose foods suited to your physical condition, and pay attention to dietary quality and diversity. God created food to nourish our bodies.

**Daily Routine**: Exercise, walk, stretch, breathe fresh air, soak in the Holy Spirit, get sufficient sleep, and declare truth.

Faith is not recklessness, nor is grace license. Stewarding the body with wisdom is in itself an act of honoring God.

## 3. Renew Your Perspective on Your Environment, and Take Up the Key of Thanksgiving

### 1. You Are an Open Gate of Heaven

"I am filled with God's love and grace every day. I influence my environment rather than being influenced by the environment around me. I am an open gate of heaven; wherever I go, I release the kingdom of God there. The light within me dispels darkness, hope overcomes discouragement, and love overcomes fear."

Your city: Connect with people of like precious faith; your transformation will impact the city. Your nation and the world: God has a plan of redemption and restoration for the entire world, and you are part of carrying out that plan.

### 2. Thanksgiving—The Key to Experiencing More Grace

Thanksgiving is our response to grace, and it is also the key to experiencing more grace. The Greek word for "thankful/thanksgiving" (εὐχάριστος, eucharistos) (original text εὐχάριστος, transliteration eucharistos; Strong's G2170; according to *Thayer's Greek Lexicon*, "thankful, grateful") shares the same root as "grace," meaning "enjoying favor—freely enjoying every good gift." When taking Communion, we express thanksgiving; in thanksgiving, we realize fellowship with God.

The practice is very concrete: whenever you experience any reduction in pain or improvement in sickness, immediately realize, "This is His healing, purchased by the price He paid." Thank Him for every measure of healing you sense, and declare every bit of improvement. Conversely, if your focus is fixed on the pain that remains, you are actually emphasizing what God has not yet accomplished.

Thanksgiving is an ongoing discipline: let us fix our eyes on the grace God has already accomplished, rather than the lack that has not yet been met. The more we give thanks, the less we dwell in lack, and the more we dwell in His faithfulness.

> "in everything give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you." (1 Thess. 5:18)

> "Rejoice in the Lord always... Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God; and the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus." (Phil. 4:6-7)

### 3. The Lesson of the Ten Lepers

Ten lepers pleaded with Jesus to have mercy on them. Jesus told them to go show themselves to the priests, and "as they went, they were cleansed." And one of them, when he saw that he was healed, returned, and with a loud voice glorified God, and fell down on his face at Jesus' feet, giving Him thanks.

All ten were healed, but only one returned to give thanks. Jesus said, "Were there not ten cleansed? But where are the nine?" (Luke 17:17)

Do not be like the nine. Be the one who returns to give thanks—those who are thankful walk into deeper grace.

## 4. Application: Six Groups of People

**1. Those who have experienced healing and fear a "relapse"**: The reappearance of disease symptoms is a lie. Use Rom. 8:11 to declare: The resurrection power lives in me, and my body will be given life. Do not let the reappearance of symptoms rob you of your faith; however, if there is a genuine abnormality in your body, seek medical attention in a timely manner to find out—standing in faith and investigating a medical condition are never enemies.

**2. Believers who are currently taking medication and seeing doctors**: Faith does not exclude wisdom. Take medication under medical supervision while giving thanks for God's healing—walking on both legs.

**3. Those who still have doubts that "God is good"**: Starting today, declare "God is good" once every day, and use thirty days to renew your mind.

**4. Co-workers involved in healing and deliverance ministry**: Remember, you are an open gate of heaven. Wherever you go, the kingdom of God is released there. Serve carrying this identity.

**5. Those who feel thanksgiving is abstract**: Starting today, keep a gratitude journal and write down one small thing God has done for you each day. The more specific your thanksgiving, the more real the healing.

**6. Those who are in good health**: Do not wait until you are sick to learn this sermon. Live out "the body is the temple of the Holy Spirit" right now, and glorify God with your health.

## Conclusion

Retaining healing is not about striving harder, but about the renewing of your mind: seeing God as good, seeing yourself as a new creation, seeing your body as the temple of the Holy Spirit, seeing your environment as a harvest field for you to impact, and finally taking up the key of thanksgiving.

## Prayer of Response

Heavenly Father, I thank You that You are a good God. I thank You that in Christ I am a new creation; old things have passed away. I thank You that my body is the temple of the Holy Spirit, and the resurrection power dwells in me. I renounce the lie that "sickness is my identity." From this day forward, I choose to give thanks for what You have already accomplished, and no longer fix my eyes on what has not yet been accomplished. I choose to give thanks continually, fixing my eyes upon the grace You have already accomplished. In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, Amen.
