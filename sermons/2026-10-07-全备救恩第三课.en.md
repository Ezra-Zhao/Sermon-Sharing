## Content

### II. My Life

(Not knowing that there is eternity — this comes from the dullness of the heart.) (Alienated from the life of God.)

1. It is brief (James 4:14) (Ps. 39:5) (Job 7:7)
2. It is illusory (Ps. 39:6) (Ps. 62:9) (Luke 12:20–21)
3. It is lowly (Ps. 103:14) (Gen. 3:19)

As for my life, it is truly brief to the point of pity, illusory to the point of misery, lowly to the point of contempt. What is man? We are soon cut off, and we fly away. (Ps. 90:10) (Isa. 40:15)

The condition of the life of the people of the world.

### III. My Living

Knowing only eating and drinking, not asking after true meaning; busy with social obligations, lacking real fellowship; weary in body and mind, with a covetousness hard to satisfy.

1. Indulging the lusts of the flesh (Eph. 2:3) (Titus 3:3) (Eph. 4:18–19)
2. Serving the belly (Rom. 16:18) (Phil. 3:18–19)
3. Minding the flesh (Rom. 8:5–6) (Rom. 8:12) (Rom. 13:14)

My whole life is nothing but seeking the pleasures of the flesh and indulging the lusts of the flesh (Eph. 2:3). The final harvest is only toil and sorrow (Ps. 90:10) and a sigh (Ps. 90:9). (Prov. 14:13: "Even in laughter the heart may sorrow, and the end of mirth may be grief.")

### IV. My Future

([Prov. 10:2] "Treasures of wickedness profit nothing, but righteousness delivers from death.")

1. Receiving condemnation (John 3:18) (Mark 16:16) (Rom. 5:18) (2 Thess. 2:12)
2. Heading toward death (Eccl. 7:2) (Eccl. 9:5)
3. Awaiting judgment (Heb. 9:27) (Ps. 98:9) (Job 34:11, 23) (Rom. 2:5–6)

When I think of my future condition, I am truly without hope. Did God create me only for me to enjoy death, to be judged and condemned, and to perish forever? (There is no one more pitiable, more miserable, more contemptible than the unbeliever.) (Rom. 14:10–12) (Matt. 12:36–37)

### Homework Questions

1. When we lived in sin, what was our standing before God?
2. Is everyone a sinner? Why?
3. What is the original substance of man as he was created?
4. As a result of sinning, what has become of the life we were created with?
5. How does the sinner live in a life that does not know God?
6. Is death man's final outcome?
7. What is the future of the sinner?
