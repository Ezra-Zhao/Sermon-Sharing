## Content

**The position of salvation — from where are we saved, and to where?** In the realization of salvation, we can see two things: God saves us from one position into another position. God's purpose is to bring us out of our original position and bring us into another position — like the Israelites being brought out of Egypt and entering Canaan. Every person who is saved should know from what condition he has been saved out, and into what condition he has been brought.

**Out of death, into life.** *Believe, and it is done:* "…and believes on Him who sent Me has everlasting life… has passed from death into life." *Hear, and live:* "…the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God; and those who hear will live." *Look, and live:* "…everyone who was bitten, when he looked at the bronze serpent, lived." In the position of salvation, the first point is passing out of death into life — dead people become living people — because God's salvation is full of power and life, making those who must die able not to die, and those who have already died able to revive. "…You have brought me up… from the gates of death" (in the original: "You have lifted me up from the gates of death").

**Out of darkness, into light.** *Turn to the light:* "…to open their eyes, in order to turn them from darkness to light." *Enter into the light:* "…who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light." *Belong to the light:* "…brothers, you are all sons of light…" (1 John — as cited in the original). To be saved is to pass out of death into life; it is also to pass out of darkness into light — that is, to be rescued out from under the power of darkness, not only to become sons of light but also to enter into God's light, so as to have fellowship with God in the light and walk with God. He is the Father of lights.

**Out of law, into grace.** *Live under grace:* "For you are not under law but under grace." *Stand in grace:* "…having been justified by faith… we have access… into this grace in which we stand." *Belong to grace:* "…that [the inheritance] is by faith, that it might be according to grace." Under the law, one relies on works; under grace, one relies on mercy. The Lord has already redeemed us out from under the law, and we belong completely to grace.

**Out of sin, into holiness.** *The unrighteous become holy:* "…the unrighteous… but now you were washed, but you were sanctified…" *The unclean become holy:* "…sprinkled on the unclean, sanctifies for the purifying of the flesh…" *The lawless become holy:* "But now… just as you presented your members as slaves of uncleanness, and of lawlessness leading to more lawlessness, so now present your members as slaves of righteousness for holiness." We are sinners by birth; naturally we live in sin, until we are full of unrighteousness, uncleanness, and lawlessness. Salvation is precisely God saving us out of all these, so that we become holy.

### Homework Questions

1. Is there a difference between the position of a sinner and the position of one who has received grace?
2. What does it mean to pass out of death into life?
3. How can one pass out of death into life?
4. What does it mean to pass out of darkness into light?
5. What does it mean to pass out of law into grace?
6. Do we still need to rely on keeping the law in order to be saved?
7. What does it mean to pass out of sin into holiness?
