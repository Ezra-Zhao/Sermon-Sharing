## Content

The center of salvation. How is the saved life? In God's complete salvation, the question of life should be the central question. After mankind fell, the essence of our life was altered, and so the character of our living became abnormal. When something went wrong with life, living developed problems. Therefore, in His way of salvation, God must solve for us this central problem of life. The rebirth, growth, and change spoken of here all pertain to this [matter of] life.

"He, according to His own will, brought us forth by the word of truth" (James 1:18, NKJV). 1. The incorruptible seed: "You have been born again, not of corruptible seed but incorruptible" (1 Pet. 1:23, NKJV). 2. Life that overcomes death: "[God] has begotten us again... through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead" (1 Pet. 1:3, NKJV). 3. Heirs of God the Father: "...to become children of God... who were born... of God" (John 1:12-13, NKJV). The so-called rebirth is not a figure of speech; it is a fact — just as our being born of our parents is a fact! This fact is that we are born of God once more. God, by the gospel and through the resurrection of Jesus Christ, has begotten us again, causing us to receive an incorruptible life, which is God's own life. Our original life was from the earth and is earthly; the reborn life, like Christ's, is from above (John) and is heavenly. The [word] in the original text has the meaning of being born from above.

"...that you may grow thereby [toward salvation]" (1 Pet. 2:2, NKJV). 1. The stature of Christ: "till we all come... to a perfect man, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ" (Eph. 4:13, NKJV). 2. Discerning good and evil: "But solid food belongs to those who are of full age... who... have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil" (Heb. 5:14, NKJV) (1 Corinthians). 3. Standing firm: "Watch, stand fast in the faith" (1 Cor. 16:13, NKJV). When God's life lives in man, it can gradually grow. [Original text partially illegible.] In the flesh [we grow], and all God's children must grow in the flesh. Rebirth means that God's life has entered into us; growth means that God's life is manifested outside of us. A reborn son may not perish; but only a grown son can be strong and victorious. Rebirth is important, and growth is even more important! Otherwise we will remain babies forever, like the believers in Corinth.

1. Changed in the twinkling of an eye: "in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet... the dead will be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed" (1 Cor. 15:52, NKJV). 2. All must be changed: "For this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality" (1 Cor. 15:53, NKJV). This concerns future salvation. Although we do not yet know how, when the Lord returns, all the saints will be changed — as glorious, as swift, and as complete as the Bible says — we do know that everyone who has God's life will experience that wonderful stage, like a metamorphosing creature that changes from a pupa into a beautiful butterfly.

Homework questions: What is the line of salvation in the aspect of life? Is [rebirth] a figure of speech, or does it refer to a fact? With what, and through what, does God cause us to be born again? Do you know that you have been born again? What is the difference between the reborn life and our original life? What is the importance of the growth of the reborn life? When can the reborn life be changed?
