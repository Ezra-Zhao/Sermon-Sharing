# Faith Q&A Question 6: Is It Possible That Jesus Will Come Again?

> **Translation status:** Initial translation, pending review by a native-speaking Christian reviewer.
> **Note:** Translated from the Chinese sermon manuscript «耶稣再来有可能吗？».

**Original work:** Rev. Stephen Tong, *Faith Q&A*, Question 6
**Adaptation:** Guangyi Zhao (retold in his own preaching style; the core views belong to the original author)

---

## Scripture

> "This Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will so come in like manner as you saw Him go into heaven." (Acts 1:11, NKJV)

> "But of that day and hour no one knows, not even the angels of heaven, but My Father only." (Matt. 24:36, NKJV)

---

## I. Not "Possible," but "Certain"

To the question "Is it possible that Jesus will come again?" Pastor Tong's answer is neat and direct: **not only is it possible; it is something that will certainly happen.**

The reason is simple: in Acts 1:11, the angels declared it in person — Jesus will come back in the same manner in which He ascended into heaven. The "manner of departure" recorded in Scripture is the manner of His "return." This is a declaration, not a guess.

## II. Who Is the One Who Comes Again: He Is "the Word"

Pastor Tong lays the foundation on "the Word": "Jesus Christ is the Word, the very One who accomplished creation and governs all things."

**Original-language analysis:** The "Word" in John 1:1 is, in Greek, **Logos (λόγος)**. In Greek thought this word denotes "the rationality of the universe, the origin of all things," and John takes it up to speak of Christ — **all things came from Him, and all things will also return to Him.**

So His coming again is not "a visitor has returned"; it is **the Origin of all things returning to settle accounts**. The first time, He came "manifested in the flesh"; the second time, He comes to "bring this world to an end and execute righteous judgment."

One more word: **"coming again" in the New Testament is *Parousia* (παρουσία)**, whose basic meaning is "presence, arrival" — when an emperor of ancient times toured into a city, this is the word that was used. The coming again of Jesus is the King of kings arriving in person, not quietly passing by.

## III. Will This World Really Have an End? Of Course It Will

Pastor Tong begins with astronomy: the sun burns millions of tons of fuel every second; burning on like this, will there be a day when it is burnt out? Of course there will.

Then he turns to the danger in man's own hands: in his day, the atomic weapons in the hands of the few nuclear powers added up to enough to destroy the world several times over. "All the missiles — press the numbers, and all the warheads fly out — may the Lord keep watch over those who press the buttons, that they do not lose their minds." (This is Pastor Tong's own sentence; I cannot resist quoting it.)

So he says: **this world is too dangerous; the possibility of its end is always on the table.**

Someone objects: doesn't the sun have another thousand or two thousand years to burn? Then I am in no hurry; take it slowly. Pastor Tong says: not necessarily. **It is very possible that God will permit man to destroy himself with his own atomic energy.** The sun in the sky will go out, and man-made weapons are even less reliable — not one excuse for "no need to be tense" holds up.

## IV. What Does He Come to Do: To Put This World in Order

Pastor Tong says that when Christ comes again, He comes to "put this world in order." He is "the Sun of Righteousness," He is "the Holy One, the Righteous One."

**Original-language analysis:** "The Sun of Righteousness" comes from Malachi 4:2; the Chinese Union Version renders it "the sun of righteousness shall arise" — the "sun" there is the sun itself. **The physical sun gives light; the Sun of Righteousness gives judgment.** He comes with light, and He also comes with fire: the light exposes everything hidden, and the fire burns away everything unrighteous.

When the First World War ended, airplanes had come out; when the Second World War ended, the atomic bomb had come out. Pastor Tong says: when the Third World War ends, everyone will be finished. **"Putting the world in order" is not an adjective; it is a verb.**

## V. When Will He Come? No One Knows

This is the most down-to-earth sentence: **perhaps tomorrow, perhaps in a hundred years; I do not know — no one knows.** Not even the Son knows; only the Father knows (Matt. 24:36).

So everyone who calculates dates, fixes years, and draws up timetables is a fraud. Pastor Tong simply does not discuss timetables with you.

## VI. Then What Should We Do Today? Go and Sow Seeds

Pastor Tong closes by quoting Augustine: **"If Jesus comes tomorrow, today I will sow my seeds just the same."** A seed sown takes until next year to sprout and grow, but today's responsibility must be completed.

We must not use "the Lord is coming soon" as a reason to lie flat and wait for death. Quite the opposite: **because the Lord is coming soon, every single seed must be sown earnestly.**

---

## For Us Today

1. The coming again of Jesus is not "possible" but "certain" — Scripture and the angels' declaration both stand there
2. The Lord who comes again is the Logos, the Origin of all things; His return is an "arrival in person" (*parousia*), not a "passing by"
3. Do not calculate dates — those who calculate dates have never once been right
4. Do today's duty well: if the Lord comes tomorrow, today we sow seeds just the same
