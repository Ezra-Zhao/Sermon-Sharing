## Content

### I. Repent Toward God

1. **The importance of repentance:** (1) Jesus personally proclaimed it (Matt. 4:17). (2) The heavenly Father's desire for man (2 Pet. 3:9). (3) All men must repent (Acts 17:30).
2. **The true meaning of repentance:** (1) Turn back and leave sin (Ezek. 18:30–32). (2) Cast away the violence in one's hands (Jonah 3:8). (3) Bear fruit worthy of repentance (Matt. 3:8).
3. **The effect of repentance:** (1) Sins are blotted out (Acts 3:19). (2) One will surely receive the Holy Spirit (Acts 2:38). (3) The angels also rejoice (Luke 15:10).

### II. Believe

1. Saved by faith (Eph. 2:8) (Mark 16:16)
2. Life by faith (John 20:31) (John 3:16)
3. Justified by faith (Acts 13:39) (Rom. 3:21–22)

Christ has come; one need only believe in Him. Scripture says: "He who believes in the Son has everlasting life" (John 3:36). Even Christ Himself said to a Samaritan woman: "…believe Me" (John 4:21 — in the original: "You should believe Me"). For God's plan is precisely to make us believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God. If we believe in this way, we can be justified by faith (Rom. 5:1) and live by faith (Gal. 3:11).

**The effects of faith:** (1) By faith, enter into rest (Heb. 4:3). (2) By faith, please God (Heb. 11:6). (3) By faith, overcome the world (1 John 5:4). (4) By faith, receive forgiveness of sins (Acts 10:43). (5) By faith, receive new life (John 20:31). (6) By faith, come out of darkness (John 12:46). (7) By faith, do great works (John 14:12). (8) By faith, stand firm (2 Cor. 1:24). (9) By faith, have great joy (1 Pet. 1:8–9). (10) By faith, be justified by God (Rom. 3:24–26).

### III. Call Upon Him

1. Ask, and it shall be given (Matt. 7:7–8) (1 John 5:14)
2. Every request is answered (1 John 5:15) (John 16:23–24)
3. Call, and be saved (Rom. 10:13) (Matt. 15:22–28)

"Believing" is the inward expression of acknowledgment; "calling" is the outward action of crying out. We see that all who called upon the Lord received mercy from the Lord. The centurion "asked" the Lord to heal his servant, and the Lord granted it to him (Matt. 9:5–13 — in the original cited as Matt. 9:5–13); the ruler of the synagogue "asked" the Lord to bring his daughter back to life, and the Lord raised her up (Matt. 9:18–25). Whoever comes to the Lord, the Lord never casts out. (John 6:37)

### Homework Questions

1. The Savior has already come; how must we treat Him in order to be saved?
2. What is the result of believing in Him?
3. What about those who do not believe?
4. What is the difference between calling and believing?
5. What is the result for those who truly call upon Him?
6. Are you willing to forsake all things and gain Christ?
7. Do you have the Son of God?
