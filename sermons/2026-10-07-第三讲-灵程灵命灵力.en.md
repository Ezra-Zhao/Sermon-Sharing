"For it is impossible for those who were once enlightened, and have tasted the heavenly gift, and have become partakers of the Holy Spirit, and have tasted the good word of God and the powers of the age to come..." (Heb. 6:4-5)

In these years of believing in the Lord, which part of your growth do you feel is the weakest? Some say: I read the Bible a lot, but my prayers have no power. Some say: I love the Lord very much, but I cannot overcome weakness. Others say: I understand all the doctrines, but I just feel "disconnected" from God.

Hidden in the verse above are three levels of a believer's growth. Brother Zhang Xun points out in the course that these are **spiritual journey, spiritual life, and spiritual power**. In the Bible, "three" represents completeness and unity — none of the three can be missing.

## I. Three Levels, None Can Be Missing

**First, the spiritual journey — "once enlightened, and have tasted the heavenly gift, and have become partakers of the Holy Spirit."**

This is the starting point. A person repents and believes in the Lord, is enlightened, tastes the heavenly gift, and becomes a partaker of the Holy Spirit — he has set out on the spiritual journey. The spiritual journey concerns **identity**: Who are you? You are a child of God; you are on the road.

**Second, spiritual life — "have tasted the good word of God."**

"The good word of God" is God's word. This course uses **logos (λόγος)** (original Greek λόγος, transliterated logos; Strong G3056; according to *Thayer's Lexicon*, "a word, the Word") to represent this piece — the written, objective true Word. Reading the Bible, studying the Bible, listening to sermons, and memorizing Scripture all build spiritual life. Frankly, ordinary believers mostly emphasize this piece, and most of our churches are strong here too. This is a good thing, but it is not enough.

**Third, spiritual power — "the powers of the age to come."**

For "the powers of the age to come," this course uses **rhēma (ῥῆμα)** (original Greek ῥῆμα, transliterated rhēma; Strong G4487; according to *Thayer's Lexicon*, "that which is spoken, a word, saying") to represent this piece — the immediate operation of the Holy Spirit here and now. Healing, deliverance, prophecy, and the spiritual warfare experienced in dreams and visions all belong to this piece. Brother Zhang Xun says plainly: **This is the piece today's church lacks most, and the piece God wants us to rise up and practice.**

(Note: In the original text of Heb. 6:5, "the good word of God" is rhēma (ῥῆμα) and "the powers of the age to come" is dynameis (δυνάμεις); this course's use of logos and rhēma to correspond respectively to spiritual life and spiritual power is an extended framework borrowing this pair of words, not a distinction in the original wording of this verse.)

It can be understood this way: the spiritual journey is "you are on the boat," spiritual life is "you can read the chart," and spiritual power is "your boat has an engine." The boat is on the water and you can read the chart, but without power the boat still cannot move. This is exactly the condition of many Christians: saved (spiritual journey), understanding the doctrines (spiritual life), but unable to experience God's great power in life (spiritual power).

What does it look like in actual life when all three are held together? The spiritual journey is confirming daily, "I am a child of God; I am on the road"; spiritual life is reading God's word daily and letting the truth shape one's thinking; spiritual power is setting aside time daily for the Holy Spirit to operate — praying in tongues, waiting on God, laying hands on those in need and praying for them. It is not choosing one of three; it is having all three. It is like a person needing air, water, and food — lacking any one of them, he will not live well.

"Three" represents completeness. Only when spiritual journey, spiritual life, and spiritual power are held together is our growth whole.

How do you know the condition of your own three pieces? You can examine yourself this way: For the spiritual journey, ask, "Am I certain that I am saved? Do I know I am in the Father's house?" For spiritual life, ask, "Do I read God's word every day? Has the truth changed my thinking?" For spiritual power, ask, "Have I experienced the operation of the Holy Spirit? Is my praying for others effective?" Whichever question you cannot answer, that piece is the one you lack most. Facing it honestly is the first step toward being made complete.

## II. A Testimony of a Dream

In the course, Brother Zhang Xun shared a dream of his own from April 23, 2007, roughly as follows:

In the dream he walked out of a building; all around was dim, and someone prophesied that he "would in the future be able to govern the wind and clouds." Then the scene shifted to a cinema, where he saw a three-dimensional vision: some people were worshiping idols, five tornadoes rushed straight toward him, he rebuked them in the name of Jesus, and the wind stopped. Afterward the cinema became a church, the words "spiritual journey, spiritual life, spiritual power" appeared in the air, and crowds of people poured in.

Miraculously, **the next day**, an American sister also received a dream, mentioning that one had to pass through three streets whose names began with the English letter S before entering a heavenly great avenue — corresponding exactly to the initials of the three English words Spiritual Journey, Spiritual Life, and Spiritual Power.

Two people separated by thousands of miles, one day apart, successively received dreams that confirmed one another. This was not a coincidence; this was God speaking.

What was received from this dream is: **The twenty-first century will be a century in which God manifests His great power through the church; the secret of the church's revival lies in holding spiritual journey, spiritual life, and spiritual power together — and spiritual power is precisely the piece most lacking today.**

This testimony is shared not for everyone to envy dreams, but for everyone to see God's heart: He wants His church to have not only the truth (spiritual life), but also power (spiritual power).

## III. The Meaning of Practicing Spiritual Power: Not Pursuing Power Itself

When we speak of "spiritual power," we must be very careful lest we go astray. Brother Zhang Xun explains the meaning very clearly in the course:

**First, practicing spiritual power is not pursuing power itself, but letting the gospel be "also in power and in the Holy Spirit."** Paul said: "For our gospel did not come to you in word only, but also in power, and in the Holy Spirit and in much assurance" (1 Thess. 1:5). Power serves the gospel; it is not for satisfying curiosity, and still less for personal fame.

**Second, the experiences of spiritual warfare in dreams and visions will expand the stature of our inner spiritual man.** Just as muscles grow under load, the stature of the spiritual man grows in real warfare and experience. God allows us to experience warfare in visions to make us grow, not to frighten us.

**Third, the training of prophetic ministry, including the practice of dreams and visions, is precisely for this.** God wants to raise up a group of people who not only understand doctrine, but who also know how to war by the Holy Spirit.

So the practice of spiritual power has a test: **Are we loving God more and loving people more?** If after practicing we become more proud and more judgmental of others, we have certainly gone astray. Power must grow in love — this is exactly what the vision of Helpers of Joy Ministries says: "perfected in love."

There is another test: Are we becoming more obedient to the Bible? The work of the Holy Spirit never contradicts the Bible. Anything that flies the banner of "the Holy Spirit's leading" yet makes people despise the Bible, despise the church, and despise a holy life is not spiritual power from God. True spiritual power makes a person love God's word more, love the church more, and thirst for holiness more.

## IV. Life Application: Six Kinds of Readers

**Those who have believed for many years and feel their growth has stalled**: Use "spiritual journey, spiritual life, spiritual power" to give yourself a checkup. Most likely your spiritual life is not lacking; what is lacking is spiritual power. Pray to God: "Lord, I am willing to be made complete by You."

**Those who emphasize only Bible study and neglect the work of the Holy Spirit**: Logos is the foundation, but do not stop there. God's word is living, and the Holy Spirit is still operating today. Ask God for an experience of rhēma.

**Those thirsting for power but in some danger of going astray**: Bring your motive before God and examine it. Do you want power in order to serve people, or in order to prove yourself? Power must grow in love.

**Those weary in serving, holding on by the flesh**: Your boat may be lacking power. Stop, and come before God to receive power afresh. Spiritual power is not a luxury; it is a necessity.

**New brothers and sisters**: From the beginning, establish a whole concept — Bible reading and prayer (spiritual life) and experiencing the Holy Spirit (spiritual power) are both needed; do not neglect either.

**Those praying for the church's revival**: The secret of revival is not livelier meetings; it is disciples who hold spiritual journey, spiritual life, and spiritual power together. Pray for this, and pray first to be made complete yourself.

## Conclusion

God's heart is complete: He wants us to have a spiritual journey (on the road), spiritual life (with the truth), and also spiritual power (with power). In the twenty-first century, God wants to manifest His great power through the church, and the starting point is that each of us is willing to have the piece we lack most made complete.

## Response Prayer

Dear Heavenly Father, thank You that Your heart is complete. Lord, we confess that we often lean toward one end: some emphasize only doctrine, others chase only experiences. Forgive us, we pray.

Lord, today we hand over to You the piece we lack most. Perhaps it is spiritual power — we are willing to practice, willing to experience Your power. But what we seek is not power itself; we seek to know You more deeply and to serve people more powerfully.

May Your great power be manifested through us, and may Your love be perfected in us. In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ we pray, Amen.
