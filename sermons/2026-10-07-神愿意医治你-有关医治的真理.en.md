Every sick person, and everyone who has ever prayed for the sick, has asked these questions in their heart:

Will God the Father heal me? Is He truly willing? Is healing God's plan for everyone, or is it a privilege reserved for only a few "spiritual giants"? If my faith isn't big enough, does that mean I cannot receive healing? I have had people pray for me many times already; is there still hope? Could God be using sickness to discipline me or teach me?

The original author, Andrew Wommack, answered these nine questions one by one in *Encounter the Healer*. This chapter does not discuss human theories; it simply looks at what the Bible says.

## I. God's Heart: He Is Willing to Heal

### 1. Look at Jesus, and You Will Know the Father's Heart

"We only need to receive the answer from Jesus, because what Jesus expressed is the very heart of God the Father."

> "I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly." (John 10:10)

> "who being the brightness of His glory and the express image of His person..." (Heb. 1:3)

Everything Jesus did was to reveal the heart of the Father—especially in healing. Jesus did only what the Father wanted Him to do, and spoke only what the Father wanted Him to speak. Of all who came to Him seeking healing, not a single one was turned away in the Gospels (Matt. 8:16; 12:15).

"For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that He might destroy the works of the devil." (1 John 3:8)

Sickness is the work of the devil; healing is the work of the Son of God. This is the Father's heart.

### 2. God's Name Is "I Am Willing"

In Mark chapter 9, the father pleaded with Jesus to heal his epileptic son; in Matthew 8:2–3, a leper came to Jesus, saying, "Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean." Then Jesus put out His hand and touched him, saying, "I am willing; be cleansed."

When you need healing, God's name is "I am willing." Stop asking, "Is God willing?" and start declaring, "God is already willing."

### 3. He Is the Same Yesterday, Today, and Forever

"Are there still miracles of healing today?"—Yes!

"Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever." (Heb. 13:8)

Just as Jesus healed the sick in biblical times, He still heals today. Moreover, "As the Father has sent Me, I also send you" (John 20:21)—He still sends us into the world today to minister healing.

## II. God's Plan: For Everyone

### 1. The Atonement Includes Your Body

> "But He was wounded for our transgressions, He was bruised for our iniquities; The chastisement for our peace was upon Him, And by His stripes we are healed." (Isa. 53:5)

> "who Himself bore our sins in His own body on the tree, that we, having died to sins, might live for righteousness—by whose stripes you were healed." (1 Pet. 2:24)

The damage sin does to our souls corresponds to the damage sickness does to our bodies. On the cross, Jesus bore both sin and sickness simultaneously—your soul is saved, and your body is also meant to be healed. These are two sides of the same salvation.

> "how God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil, for God was with Him." (Acts 10:38)

### 2. By Grace, Not by Works—Little Faith Is Not a Hindrance

"Do I have enough faith? I don't feel like I have enough faith..."

Jesus is the author and finisher of our faith. In Scripture, regardless of the measure of faith the sick person had, Jesus healed them: Lazarus in John chapter 11 (who was already dead and had no faith to speak of), Tabitha (Dorcas) in Acts chapter 9, the epileptic boy in Mark chapter 9 (whose father said, "Lord, I believe; help my unbelief!"), and the leper in Mark chapter 1.

"I feel like I'm not good enough; maybe my sickness is my own fault..."—

> "For by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, lest anyone should boast." (Eph. 2:8)

Healing, just like salvation, is by grace. Before healing someone, Jesus never first counted up their sins (cf. the paralytic at the Pool of Bethesda in John 5). "For God did not send His Son into the world to condemn the world, but that the world through Him might be saved." (John 3:17) God is not afraid of your sin; sin cannot stop Him from coming to heal you.

### 3. Sickness Is Not God's Instrument

"Will God use sickness to teach me and make me complete? Will God be glorified through it?"

No. The position of this chapter is: God has never used sickness to teach people, nor has He ever withheld healing to build up someone's life. God does work through the circumstances of our lives to build us up (Rom. 8:28), but sickness and pain are not God's instruments, much less His gifts—only healing is His gift. (It should be noted that there have historically been different interpretations of passages such as Paul's thorn in the flesh in 2 Cor. 12:7–9; what is presented here is the position of this chapter, and readers may examine the scriptures and judge for themselves.)

Think about it: what kind of parent would make their child sick and physically weak just to teach them a life lesson? Our Heavenly Father is far better than earthly parents.

## III. Receiving Healing: Now Is the Time

### 1. Now Is the Accepted Time

"Is now the time for me to be healed? Or do I have to wait for the future?"

Jesus never told anyone to go away and come back for healing at a more suitable time. He even healed on the Sabbath—which, in the eyes of the religious leaders of that day, was exceedingly "untimely."

"Behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation." (2 Cor. 6:2)

Do not wait. Healing has no "off-season."

### 2. After Being Prayed for Many Times, There Is Still Hope

"I have already received prayer many times..."

Healing does not depend on the anointing of man, but on the anointing of God. No matter how many times people have prayed for you, the point is this: God wants to meet you today, and He wants to heal you.

> "This I recall to my mind, Therefore I have hope. Through the Lord's mercies we are not consumed, Because His compassions fail not. They are new every morning; Great is Your faithfulness. 'The Lord is my portion,' says my soul, 'Therefore I hope in Him!'" (Lam. 3:21–24)

Everything before this moment has already passed away (2 Cor. 5:17). They are new every morning—including this morning.

### 3. Sickness Is Not Your Identity

"Is sickness my identity?"

Even if you are currently in a certain condition, that does not mean you will be trapped in it forever. Jesus' ministry was to bring heaven to earth; in heaven, there is no sickness, pain, or depression.

"Your kingdom come. Your will be done On earth as it is in heaven." (Matt. 6:10)

"I have been crucified with Christ; it is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me." (Gal. 2:20)

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new." (2 Cor. 5:17)

Your identity is in Jesus Christ, not in your medical record. Starting today, stop defining yourself by sickness.

## IV. Application: Six Groups of People

**1. Brothers and sisters currently in pain and sickness**: Today God says to you, "I am willing." Stop asking if God is willing; start thanking Him for the healing that is already accomplished.

**2. Those who have sought healing many times without receiving it and are beginning to lose heart**: They are new every morning. None of your past prayers were in vain; today is a new beginning—look to Him afresh.

**3. Those who feel they are not good enough or unworthy to be healed**: Healing is by grace, not by works. The paralytic at Bethesda had been sick for thirty-eight years, yet Jesus healed him just the same.

**4. Co-workers who intercede for the sick and are involved in healing ministry**: Remember John 20:21—as the Father sent Jesus, so He sends you. Go, and minister healing in the name of the Lord.

**5. Brothers and sisters who believe that "God uses sickness to teach people"**: Nail that concept to the cross. God is a good Father; He gives only good things to His children.

**6. Seekers in good health who have come to accompany the sick**: What you have heard today is about a God who is willing to heal. Such a God is worthy of your knowing, worthy of your trust.

## Conclusion

To nine questions, the Bible gives one single answer: God is willing, God has already planned it, and God is ready to do it right now.

## Response Prayer

Heavenly Father, I thank You that You sent Jesus to be wounded with stripes for me so that I might be healed. I renounce the lies that "I am not worthy," "the time has not yet come," and "God wants to use sickness to teach me." I receive Your "I am willing." I declare: sickness is not my identity; I am a new creation in Christ. Now is the accepted time; now is the day of salvation. In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, Amen.
