# Faith Q&A Question 1: What Exactly Is the Will of God?

## Scripture

> "If anyone wills to do His will, he shall know concerning the doctrine, whether it is from God or whether I speak on My own authority." (John 7:17, NKJV)

> "For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God." (Rom. 8:14, NKJV)

---

## I. Do Not Keep the Words "the Will of God" on Your Lips

Pastor Tong pours cold water right at the start: **a person who says "this is the will of God" about everything proves precisely that he does not really understand what the will of God is.**

Why? Because the four words "the will of God" are used with extreme care in Scripture — even apostles and prophets did not dare use them lightly. A person who truly fears God will not open and close his mouth with "the will of God."

There is a bad habit in the church today: looking for a job is "the will of God," dating is "the will of God," even what to eat for lunch is "the will of God." Pastor Tong's point is this: **the more readily a person says it, the less he understands the weight of these words.**

---

## II. Will vs. Guidance: Two Different Things

This is the core of the whole piece, and Pastor Tong draws the distinction very clearly:

**The will of God (Eternal decree):** eternal, unchanging, and the same for everyone.
- Example: God wants every Christian to preach the gospel; God wants us to be holy — this is His will, and everyone has a share in it.

**The guidance of God (Dynamic process):** at work in history, differing from person to person, and changing with the times.
- Example: God guides you to become a full-time preacher, and guides him to serve while keeping a secular job — this is guidance, and it differs for each person.

**The key:** Do not take guidance and impose it on others as if it were God's will.
- If you are guided to remain single, do not say "God's will is for everyone to remain single" — by that measure even Jesus would have "violated" it (He did not marry either, yet He fully carried out God's will).
- If you are guided to marry, do not say "not marrying is rebellion against God" — Paul remained single, yet he was a servant greatly used by God.

In one sentence: **the will is the same for everyone; guidance differs for each person.**

---

## III. To Whom Is the Will Revealed?

John 7:17 gives the answer: "If anyone **wills to do** His will, he shall know."

The order is: **will → do → know**.
- First resolve to obey
- Then actually carry it out
- Only then will you know

Many people reverse the order: they want to know first, and only then decide whether to obey. Pastor Tong says: **God's will is not revealed to a person who has no intention of obeying.** If you have not yet decided to obey, why should God tell you?

---

## IV. For Us Today

1. Say "the will of God" less, and ask "Have I obeyed?" more
2. Do not use your own guidance to bind others
3. Want to know God's will? Resolve to obey first; the answer lies on the far side of obedience
