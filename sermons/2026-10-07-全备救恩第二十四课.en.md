## Content

The carrying out of salvation. What is God's action in saving us? When God brings us into salvation, God uses His saving power to carry out the way of salvation. We are not [saved by ourselves — original text partially illegible]. Let us ask: when we were saved, what was God's action in saving us? The Bible uses many words and phrases concerning God's actions in salvation; all of them are God's actions in salvation. If we were to speak of them one by one in detail, the time would not be enough. We therefore select the representative ones and list them as follows.

Lifting up / delivering ("He delivered me, because He delighted in me" (Ps. 18:19 (Eng. 18:19/ Heb. v.20 in some editions); cf. 2 Sam. 22:20, NKJV)) (this is position). 1. The object of this delivering: "He does not deliver angels, but He does deliver the seed of Abraham" (Heb. 2:16, NKJV). 2. The nature of this delivering: "[He] lifts the poor out of the dust, and raises the needy from the ash heap" (cf. Ps. 113:7, NKJV). 3. The meaning of this delivering: "He also brought me up out of a horrible pit, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock" (Ps. 40:2, NKJV). 4. The effect of this delivering: calling them "sons of the living God" (Rom. 9:26; Hos. 1:10, NKJV). The action of delivering has the meaning of lifting up and pulling up. This pulling up is not only from dust to a throne and from mire to a rock; it causes the creature to share in the Creator. Man, in his original position, even if he had not fallen, would still need God's delivering! For even if Adam had not failed, he would still only be a sinless man. Today, because of God's delivering, we have become sons of the living God. "The Lord knows how to deliver the godly out of temptations" (2 Pet. 2:9, NKJV).

Rescuing (this is experience and testimony). 1. Delivered from temptation: "For in that He Himself has suffered, being tempted, He is able to aid those who are tempted" (Heb. 2:18, NKJV). 2. Delivered from tearing: "Lest they tear me like a lion, rending me in pieces, while there is none to deliver" (Ps. 7:2, NKJV). 3. Delivered from trouble: "He delivered them out of their distresses" (Ps. 107, NKJV). 4. Delivered from enemies: "Their enemies also oppressed them... Many times He delivered them" (Ps. 106:42-43, NKJV). God's action of rescuing, in the Bible's usage, mostly refers to present temptations and evils. Temptation concerns damage to the spiritual life; evil concerns harm to life itself. Thank God: in our experience of grace, He has rescued us again and again, and He rescues us continually.

Saving ("For You will answer us, God of our salvation, with awesome deeds in righteousness" — cf. Ps. 65:5, NKJV) (this is future). 1. Future saving: "He will appear a second time... for salvation, to those who eagerly wait for Him" (Heb. 9:28, NKJV). 2. Saving to the uttermost: "Therefore He is also able to save to the uttermost those who come to God through Him" (Heb. 7:25, NKJV). The action of saving is a continuing action of God in salvation; it can be applied to both present and future. Used of the past: "The Son of Man has come to seek and to save that which was lost" (Luke 19:10, NKJV). Used of the present: "He saved them out of their distresses" (Ps. 107, NKJV). Used of the future: appearing a second time to save them. This is not only a continuing action; it is also a thoroughgoing, consistent action! For "He is also able to save to the uttermost those who come to God through Him" (Heb. 7:25, NKJV)! ("For He says: 'In an acceptable time I have heard you, and in the day of salvation I have helped you.' Behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation" (2 Cor. 6:2, NKJV).)

Homework questions: Try to give three of God's actions in saving us. What does delivering/lifting up mean? To what extent has God lifted us up? God's action of [saving], in today's salvation, refers to which two aspects? What is the difference between these two aspects?
