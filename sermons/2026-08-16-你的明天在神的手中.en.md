## Full Text

Sermon transcript: *Your Tomorrow Is in God's Hands* — Speaker: Guangyi — Date: August 16, 2026 — Core Scriptures: Joshua 3:5; 4:20-24; Acts 1:8; Matthew 6:34 — Speaking length: about 60 minutes (in-depth expository and comprehensive application version)

### (00:00 - 08:00) Opening

Brothers and sisters, peace to you all! I am very glad to gather and worship with you today in the house of the Lord.

The theme I want to share with you today is: *Your Tomorrow Is in God's Hands*.

Please think about this: when we face an unknown tomorrow, what is the feeling we most often have in our hearts? Very often it is panic — anxiety over uncertainty. The inner drain modern people fall into most easily is worry about "tomorrow." What will tomorrow's work be like? What will tomorrow's economy be like? What will tomorrow's health be like? What will tomorrow's family and children be like?

In Matthew chapter 6, verse 34, the Lord Jesus Himself says to us: "Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about its own things. Sufficient for the day is its own trouble." (Matt. 6:34, NKJV)

**Scripture Exposition 1: Matthew 6:34 (Do not worry about tomorrow)**

Word study: The Greek word for "worry" is *Merimnaō* (transliteration: me-rim-na-ō), derived from *Merizō* (to divide, to tear apart) and *Nous* (the mind). The literal meaning of this word is "the mind being torn apart, being pulled in different directions."

Exposition and spiritual mechanism: The Lord Jesus pointed out the essence of anxiety — when your mind is torn apart (*Merimnaō*) by the unknowns of tomorrow, you not only fail to solve tomorrow's problems, you also lose the peace of today. The Lord Jesus is not telling us to be completely irresponsible about life; He is telling us a spiritual fact of the kingdom of heaven: your tomorrow is not in the hands of your circumstances; your tomorrow is not in the hands of worldly trends; your tomorrow is held tightly in the hands of Almighty God!

When the Israelites were preparing to cross the Jordan River and enter the land of Canaan, they too were facing a completely unknown tomorrow. They did not know how strong the walls ahead of them were, and they did not know how mighty the giants ahead of them were. But Joshua said one sentence to the people: "Sanctify yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you." (Josh. 3:5, NKJV)

Brothers and sisters, when we are about to cross the Jordan River of our lives and walk toward an unknown tomorrow, how should we prepare ourselves? Today we will look at three extremely important spiritual principles.

### (08:00 - 28:00) Part One: Remember God's Faithfulness

To walk into God's tomorrow, the first extremely important thing is to learn to "remember God's faithfulness."

Please look at Joshua chapter 4, verses 20 to 24. When the Israelites obeyed God's command and set foot into the waters of the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan were cut off before them, and the people walked across on dry ground. After they had all crossed the Jordan, God commanded Joshua to do a very special thing — to have representatives of the twelve tribes take twelve stones from the bottom of the Jordan and set them up at Gilgal.

Why did God have them set up these twelve stones? Verses 21 to 22 say: "Then he spoke to the children of Israel, saying: 'When your children ask their fathers in time to come, saying, "What are these stones?" then you shall let your children know, saying, "Israel crossed over this Jordan on dry land."'" (Josh. 4:21-22, NKJV)

**Scripture Exposition 2: Joshua 4:21-22 (The stones at Gilgal and remembrance)**

Word study: The Hebrew word for "remember" is *Zakar* (transliteration: za-kar). In the Old Testament, *Zakar* is by no means merely a recollection in the mind; it is an action of "activating present obedience and trust on the basis of past facts of faithfulness."

Exposition and spiritual mechanism: The stones at Gilgal are the spiritual anchor point of *Zakar*. The reason many people panic about tomorrow is that they suffer from a spiritual "amnesia." When danger and challenges come, we immediately forget God's grace toward us yesterday. God had the Israelites set up stones so that these twelve stones would become a visual testimony of *Zakar*: the God who made a way at the Red Sea and at the Jordan yesterday will also make a way in the land of Canaan today!

**Corresponding Psalm passage and exposition**

Corresponding Scripture: "I will remember the works of the LORD; surely I will remember Your wonders of old. I will also meditate on all Your work, and talk of Your deeds." (Ps. 77:11-12, NKJV)

Exposition and connection: Psalm 77 was written by the psalmist Asaph in extreme pain and sleeplessness. When he saw that in reality there was no way forward, the secret that saved his heart was to "remember (*Zakar*) the works of the LORD." Remembering God's faithfulness can pour into our hearts a peace like an anchor that holds the sea still.

**Application in the present-day realities of China**

In the various roles of today's Chinese society, "remembering God's faithfulness" can bring great power to break through:

- **Workplace / workers (facing fear of pay cuts and layoffs):** Facing layoffs at large companies or industry adjustments, do not fall into worldly inner drain. Take out paper and pen, and write down how God made a way for you in several workplace crises over the past few years. Remembering (*Zakar*) God's past provision can help you shift your gaze from panicked unemployment reports to the mighty God, so that you keep a steady, anchored mindset in interviews and transitions.
- **Business / entrepreneurs (facing cash flow and market risks):** Brothers in business face a contracting market and are anxious every day about tomorrow's cash flow. Recall how God prepared resources from nothing at the beginning of your venture. Remembering God's faithfulness will remind you: success or failure in business does not lie in worldly waves, but in the mighty care of Almighty God.
- **The sick / those whose health is being tested:** When you receive a diagnosis of a serious illness, fear tears the mind apart in an instant. Remember every healing and protection God has given you in body and spirit in the past, build the "stones of Gilgal" of your life, and look only to God's grace in the midst of illness.

### (28:00 - 48:00) Part Two: Rely Anew on the Power of the Holy Spirit

Second, to welcome God's tomorrow, we must "rely anew on the power of the Holy Spirit."

After the Israelites crossed the Jordan and entered the land of Canaan, the old pattern stopped — the manna no longer fell, and they had to face the fortified city of Jericho. Facing completely new and unknown challenges, the principle God gives us is not to rely on human flesh and cleverness, but on the power of the Holy Spirit.

Acts chapter 1, verse 8 says: "But you shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you; and you shall be witnesses to Me in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth." (Acts 1:8, NKJV)

**Scripture Exposition 3: Acts 1:8 (The Holy Spirit descends and we receive power)**

Word study: The Greek word for "power" is *Dunamis* (transliteration: du-na-mis), from which the English word "dynamite" is derived. This word refers to "explosive, supernatural power," distinct from the physical strength or intellect (*Ischys*) that belongs to the human body itself.

Exposition and spiritual mechanism: The disciples at that time were facing persecution from the Roman Empire and an unknown tomorrow. If they had relied on their own flesh and experience, they could not possibly have changed the world. But when the Holy Spirit descended at Pentecost and gave supernatural *Dunamis* power, a group of weak and timid fishermen became gospel warriors who turned their era upside down. To enter tomorrow, we cannot act by inertia; we must seek afresh every day to be filled with *Dunamis*.

**Corresponding Proverbs passage and exposition**

Corresponding Scripture: "Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths." (Prov. 3:5-6, NKJV)

Exposition and connection: "Leaning on your own understanding" is acting by the inertia of the flesh, while "trusting with all your heart" is opening yourself to the power of the Holy Spirit (*Dunamis*). When you no longer prop yourself up with worldly cleverness, the transcendent wisdom and guidance of the Holy Spirit will be clearly revealed.

**Application in the present-day realities of China**

Facing complex interpersonal and social tensions, "relying anew on the power of the Holy Spirit" is the source of strength for every role:

- **Marital relationships (breaking cold wars and communication deadlocks):** In marriage, facing friction of personalities and family financial pressure, arguing in the flesh only hurts each other. Give up managing the other person by experience; ask the Holy Spirit's *Dunamis* to blast open the pride and hardness in the heart, and to give a love and forbearance that go beyond the limits of the flesh, to save a broken marriage.
- **Mother-in-law / daughter-in-law relationships (facing child-rearing and viewpoint conflicts):** Two generations have a huge gap in living habits and ideas about raising children, and opposition easily arises. Do not wrestle in your own flesh; in prayer ask the Holy Spirit to give gentleness and wisdom that go beyond yourself (Prov. 3:5-6), and resolve tension with gentle communication (a soft answer).
- **The elderly and those caring for incapacitated elders:** Facing an elderly person with Alzheimer's disease or who is bedridden long-term, caregivers easily become exhausted and lose emotional control. Relying on limited human physical strength and patience will eventually run dry; ask the Holy Spirit every day to give *Dunamis* supernatural endurance and gentleness, so that even in extremely weary bedside care, the rest of the kingdom of heaven flows out.

### (48:00 - 58:00) Part Three: Declare — My Tomorrow Is in God's Hands!

Third, we must bravely "declare: My tomorrow is in God's hands!"

Please note, this is not merely an inner belief; it is even more a declaration of the mouth and an obedience of life.

Joshua declared before the people: "Sanctify yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you." (Josh. 3:5, NKJV)

**Scripture Exposition 4: Joshua 3:5 (Sanctify yourselves and do wonders)**

Word study: The Hebrew word for "sanctify" is *Kadosh* (transliteration: ka-dosh), meaning "to be set apart, consecrated to God"; the Hebrew word for "do wonders" is *Niphlā'ōt* (transliteration: niph-la-oth), referring to miracles beyond the laws of nature that only God can accomplish.

Exposition and spiritual mechanism: Joshua required the people first to carry out *Kadosh* (sanctify themselves / hand control back to God), and only afterward would God perform *Niphlā'ōt* (wonders). Very often the reason we do not receive God's wonderful leading is that we cling tightly to control and refuse to let go. A true declaration is to take the initiative in *Kadosh* — to surrender completely the control of tomorrow to God, and thereby see God perform *Niphlā'ōt* tomorrow.

**Corresponding Proverbs and Psalm passages and exposition**

Corresponding Scripture: "Death and life are in the power of the tongue, and those who love it will eat its fruit." (Prov. 18:21, NKJV)

Corresponding Scripture: "But as for me, I trust in You, O LORD; I say, 'You are my God!' My times are in Your hand." (Ps. 31:14-15, NKJV)

Exposition and connection: Your mouth has authority. If every day you casually declare "It's all over, there's no hope for the future," you are handing control over to panic; but when, like Psalm 31, you declare aloud "My tomorrow is in Your hand," you are using the authority of the tongue (Prov. 18:21) to anchor your life in the faithfulness of the heavenly Father.

**Application in the present-day realities of China**

- **Children and parents (breaking educational involution and anxiety):** Facing the pressure of advancement to higher schools and the storm of involution, parents easily pour their fear of the future into their children. Parents must stop anxious accusations from their mouths, and bravely declare before their children, "The child's tomorrow is in God's hands" (Ps. 31:15). Hand control completely back to God (*Kadosh*), no longer compare your child with the world, and accompany your child to grow according to the unique gifts God has given.

### (58:00 - 60:00) Conclusion and Closing Prayer

Brothers and sisters, no matter what Jordan River you are facing today, no matter how much unease and anxiety you have about the unknown tomorrow, please remember these three spiritual principles:

1. **Remember God's faithfulness:** Look back at the "stones of Gilgal" (*Zakar*) God has set up for you in the past;
2. **Rely anew on the power of the Holy Spirit:** Lay down your self-righteous flesh and cleverness (*Dunamis*);
3. **Declare that tomorrow is in God's hands:** By faith, hand the control of tomorrow completely back to the Lord (*Kadosh*), and bravely cross the river and move forward!

Your tomorrow is not in darkness, your tomorrow is not in panic, your tomorrow is in the gracious hand of Almighty God!

"Dear heavenly Father, we thank You! For You are the God who does not change — the same yesterday, today, and forever.

Lord, today we come before You and lay down before You all the panic in our hearts about the unknown and all our anxiety about tomorrow. Lord, please forgive us that we so often suffer from spiritual amnesia and forget Your faithful grace in the past, when You parted the Red Sea and led us across the Jordan in our lives.

Today, we will build stones of remembrance (*Zakar*) before You! We will look to You anew. May Your Holy Spirit descend upon every brother and sister, give *Dunamis* power to fill us anew, remove the timidity and inner drain within us, and give us a spirit of power and of love and of a sound mind.

Today we will declare in the Spirit to all the earth: Our tomorrow is not in the hands of circumstances, our tomorrow is not in the hands of human favor, our tomorrow is held tightly in Your mighty and gracious hand, heavenly Father (Ps. 31:15)!

Thank You, Lord, for hearing our prayer. In the precious and powerful name of the Lord Jesus Christ we pray, Amen!"
