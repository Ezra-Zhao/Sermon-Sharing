## Content

### II. What About Doing Righteousness?

1. Filthy and defiled (Isa. 64:6) (Hag. 2:11–14)
2. Powerless to do righteousness (Rom. 8:7) (Gal. 3:11)
3. Afraid that God will call us to account (Ps. 143:2) (Ps. 130:3)

The Israelites once paid an enormous price: "Israel, pursuing the law of righteousness, has not attained to the law of righteousness" (Rom. 9:31). For "if righteousness comes through the law, then Christ died in vain" (Gal. 2:21). The failure of the Israelites was precisely because they wanted to establish their own righteousness by the law (Rom. 10:3), and so they did not submit to the righteousness of God. (Eccl. 7:20) (Jer. 13:23) (Gal. 2:21)

### III. What About Striving?

1. Running is in vain (Rom. 9:11–18) (Jer. 17:5–8) (Jer. 10:23)
2. Running swiftly is of no avail (Eccl. 9:11) (Ps. 147:10) (Prov. 21:31)

For man to use his own methods and rely on his own striving to reach the goal of being saved is forever impossible. For salvation is not something we accomplish ourselves! Apart from God's grace and mercy, all our running is in vain.

### IV. What About Asceticism?

1. Legalism of rules (Col. 2:20–22) (1 Tim. 4:1–3)
2. Asceticism (Col. 2:23) (Rom. 2:28) (Phil. 3:2–3) (Gal. 5:1)

Man's corruption comes from the inheritance of his nature. One can never, by means of some rules and restraints, reach the goal of being saved. [Prov. 14:12] "There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death."

### Homework Questions

1. When you have understood the condition of human life, do you long to be saved?
2. Can you be saved by resolving to do good? Why?
3. How does our doing of righteousness appear before God?
4. Can a man be justified by keeping the law?
5. What about striving desperately and pursuing salvation?
6. What about strictly keeping rules?
7. What about afflicting oneself and restraining the flesh?

God's eternal plan: Eternity — Creation — the Fall — Redemption — Dispensing — Eternity (Eph. 1:5). One Son — many sons (Rom. 8:30) (Rom. 8:19) (Heb. 2:10). Predestined — Called — Justified — Glorified. Through the preaching of the gospel, the sons of God are manifested (John 6:37–40) (2 Tim. 1:9) (Rev. 22:5) (Ps. 113:7–8). Plan — Types — Prophecies — Accomplishment — Fulfillment — Implementation.
