## (08:00 - 25:00) Part 1: God Saw That It Was Good—God's Evaluation and Separation

### 1.1 What Does "Good" Mean in the Original Text?

Verse 4: "And God saw the light, that it was good."

The Hebrew word for "good" is **Tov** (טוֹב). This word does not simply mean "not bad"; it means: good, beautiful, noble, delightful—it is God's own personal seal of approval!

Brothers and sisters, notice a detail: in Genesis chapter 1, "God saw that it was good" appears a total of **seven times**! Verse 4, verse 10, verse 12, verse 18, verse 21, verse 25—six times "God saw that it was good," and finally verse 31, God looked at everything He had made, and "indeed it was **very good**"—the seventh time!

Seven, in Scripture, is the number of completeness. God's evaluation of His creation begins with "good" and ends with "very good"—**God's creation is completely good!** [Emphasis]

English Version Comparison:
- KJV: "And God saw the light, that **it was good**."
- NIV: "God saw that the light **was good**."
- Lü Zhenzhong Translation: "God saw that the light was **good**."

### 1.2 What Does "Divided" Mean in the Original Text?

The latter half of verse 4: "and God divided the light from the darkness."

The Hebrew word for "divided" is **Badal** (בָּדַל)—to separate, set apart, make a distinction.

Brothers and sisters, the first action of God's creation was not "mixing," it was "separating"! Light and darkness cannot be mixed together; if they are mixed together, they become indistinguishable. God separated light from darkness, letting light belong to light and darkness belong to darkness, each returning to its proper place.

"God called the light Day, and the darkness He called Night"—"called," in Hebrew, is **Qara** (קָרָא), which carries the meaning of "naming, declaring sovereignty." God naming the light was equivalent to declaring: **Sovereignty over day and night is in My hands!**

### 1.3 Golden Scripture Passages and Word Studies

**"For every creature of God is good, and nothing is to be refused if it is received with thanksgiving; for it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer." (1 Tim. 4:4-5)**

Original Word Study: Paul directly brings the Genesis **Tov** right onto the dining table here! "Every creature of God is good"—the Greek word for "good" is **Kalos**, which shares the same root used for "good" in the Septuagint translation of Genesis 1. Paul's meaning is: what God called "good" in the beginning is still "good" today—when you receive it with thanksgiving, it becomes sanctified!

Pulpit Spoken Explication: A single grain of rice you eat today, a mouthful of water you drink, a breath of air you inhale, God saw it as "good" in the beginning. Do not complain while you eat; receive it with thanksgiving—gratitude turns "good" into "sanctified"!

---

**"For the Lord does not see as man sees; for man looks at the outward appearance, but the Lord looks at the heart." (1 Sam. 16:7)**

Pulpit Spoken Explication: Human evaluation looks at outward appearance: it looks at academic degrees, looks at looks, looks at accents, looks at status, looks at bank balances. God's evaluation looks at the heart. **Man says you are "no good," but God says you are Tov!** Whose voice are you going to listen to?

---

**"I will praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made; marvelous are Your works, and that my soul knows very well." (Ps. 139:13-14)**

Original Word Study: The Hebrew for "fearfully" comes from **Yare** (יָרֵא), which carries the sense of "inspiring awe." David said: I am "fearfully and wonderfully made"—even when I look at myself, I feel astonished!

Pulpit Spoken Explication: David was not just feeling good about himself; he had come to understand: **I am a masterpiece upon which God looked and said "good"!** When you look in the mirror, do not start by finding flaws; start by saying: "God saw that I was good!"

---

**"Then God saw everything that He had made, and indeed it was very good." (Gen. 1:31)**

Pulpit Spoken Explication: Notice "everything"—no exceptions! God did not say, "this is good, but that one is so-so." **Everything He made was very good, and that includes you!**

### 1.4 Biblical Example: Samuel Anointing David

In the Old Testament, there is a story that illustrates best just how far apart "God's evaluation" and "man's evaluation" really are.

Samuel was commanded by God to go to Bethlehem to anoint one of Jesse's sons as king. Jesse's eldest son, Eliab, walked out—tall and handsome, of "fine appearance." Samuel thought in his heart, "Surely this must be the Lord's anointed!"

Yet God said: "**Do not look at his appearance or at his physical stature, because I have refused him. For the Lord does not see as man sees; for man looks at the outward appearance, but the Lord looks at the heart.**" (1 Sam. 16:7)

Seven sons walked by one by one, and to every single one God said "no." Finally, Jesse called back his youngest son David from out in the field tending sheep—a little shepherd boy smelling all over of sheep. God said: "**Arise, anoint him; for this is the one!**" (1 Sam. 16:12)

Brothers and sisters, when Samuel looked at Eliab, his eyes said "good"; but when God looked at Eliab, His heart said "no." When people looked at David, their eyes said "he's just a shepherd boy"; but when God looked at David, His heart said "this is the one"!

[Pause for 2 seconds]

How many people today are living in the evaluation of others? Your boss says you are inadequate, so you feel inadequate; other people's children excel at everything, so you feel your own are completely worthless; everyone on social media looks glamorous, so you feel your own life is an utter mess.

But God says: **My evaluation is the final verdict!** If I say you are Tov, then you are Tov!

## (25:00 - 42:00) Part Two: The Firmament and the Dry Land—The Boundaries God Established

### 2.1 What Does "Firmament" Mean in the Original Text?

Verse 6: "Then God said, 'Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.'" (Gen. 1:6)

The Chinese Union Version adds a marginal note for "air," reading "literally firmament." The Hebrew word for firmament is **Raqia** (רָקִיעַ)—this word originally means "a beaten-out expanse or dome," like a blacksmith beating out metal into an expansive canopy.

God used the *Raqia* to divide the waters: the waters which were above the firmament from the waters which were under the firmament. In between is the air we breathe, the sky where birds can fly.

Brothers and sisters, into the chaotic waters, God drove a **boundary**! The waters were no longer a boundless, engulfing ocean; they now had above, below, and in-between—they had order, and they had space.

In verse 9, God made another separation: "Let the waters under the heavens be gathered together into one place, and let the dry land appear." (Gen. 1:9) The waters gathered into seas, and the dry land appeared—the seas gained shorelines, and the land gained borders.

### 2.2 God Sets Boundaries for the Sea

There is a passage in Scripture where God Himself delivers a "discourse on boundaries," found in Job 38:

"**Or who shut in the sea with doors, when it burst forth and issued from the womb? ... When I fixed My limit for it, and set bars and doors; when I said, 'This far you may come, but no farther, and here your proud waves must stop!'**" (Job 38:8, 10-11, excerpted)

Brothers and sisters, read this passage with imagery in your mind: the sea bursts forth like a newborn baby from the womb, its proud waves seeking to engulf everything. God says, "Stop! I set doors and bars for you. This far you may come, but no farther!"

"Set bars and doors"—God installed doors for the sea! No matter how raging the ocean gets, it cannot cross the boundary established by God.

English comparison:
- KJV: "**Hitherto shalt thou come, but no further**: and here shall thy proud waves be stayed."
- NIV: "This far you may come **and no farther**; here is where your proud waves halt."
- Lu Zhenzhong Version: "This far you may come, and not cross over; here shall your proud waves halt."

Jeremiah also says: "**I have placed the sand as the bound of the sea**" (Jer. 5:22)—God restrained the proudest sea with the softest sand! Grains of sand are tiny and frail, yet when God declares, "This is the boundary," the ocean obediently halts.

### 2.3 Golden Scripture Passages and Original Word Analysis

**"He lays the beams of His upper chambers in the waters... You have set a boundary that they may not pass over, that they may not return to cover the earth." (Ps. 104:3, 9, excerpted)**

Pulpit oral exposition: Psalm 104 is the longest creation psalm in the Bible. The psalmist says: God "set a boundary," so the waters "may not pass over." **The moment a boundary is established, a deluge becomes rivers, lakes, and oceans—transformed from a catastrophe into a resource!** The very same water: without boundaries, it is a flood; with boundaries, it is provision. Brothers and sisters, those things "spilling over" in your life—emotions, desires, busyness, smartphones—what they lack is not water; what they lack is a boundary!

---

**"When He prepared the heavens, I was there, when He drew a circle on the face of the deep, when He established the clouds above, when He strengthened the fountains of the deep, when He assigned to the sea its limit, so that the waters would not transgress His command." (Prov. 8:27-29, excerpted)**

Original word analysis: The "I" here is **Wisdom**. Proverbs says that when God established the boundaries, Wisdom was present—"drew a circle" and "assigned to the sea its limit" are both the actions of wisdom.

Pulpit oral exposition: A boundary was not drawn casually by God; it was **drawn according to wisdom**! Every circle God draws around your life—time, marriage, finances, physical health—is a design of wisdom. When you guard your boundaries, you are walking in wisdom.

---

**"Then God said, 'Let the earth bring forth grass, the herb that yields seed, and the fruit tree that yields fruit according to its kind, whose seed is in itself, on the earth'; and it was so." (Gen. 1:11)**

Pulpit oral exposition: Notice the sequence: **First come the boundaries (the dry land appears), and then comes the fruitfulness (the earth brings forth vegetation)!** Without dry land, where would you sow the seed? Brothers and sisters, God first establishes boundaries for you, and then He pours out blessings—boundaries are the fertile soil for blessings!

### 2.4 Biblical Example: The Boundaries of Manna

In Exodus 16, God rained down manna from heaven for the Israelites to eat. The manna was free, daily, and sufficient to satisfy—yet God set a rule:

"Gather it, every man according to his need; you shall not gather too much, nor too little; **let no one leave any of it till morning.**" (Ex. 16:16, 19, paraphrased)

Some did not listen and insisted on keeping some until morning; the result was that it "**bred worms and stank**" (Ex. 16:20).

On the Sabbath, God said no manna would fall that day, so they were to gather a double portion on the sixth day. Yet some still went out on the Sabbath to look for it, and "**they found none**" (Ex. 16:27).

Brothers and sisters, do you see? God gives blessings, but He also sets boundaries. Boundaries are not stinginess; they are protection:

- Gathering daily—teaches you to **depend on God every day**, without hoarding and without anxiety;
- Not leaving any till morning—teaches you **not to be greedy**; greed breeds rot and stench;
- Resting on the Sabbath—teaches you to **stop**, for even God Himself rested on the seventh day.

[Pause 2 seconds]

How many people today have consumed their "blessings" until they have "bred worms and stank"? With more money, relationships turn foul; with more busyness, health deteriorates; with more desires, life rots. The blessing itself wasn't wrong; **the boundaries were lost**!

God set a boundary for the ocean, and He sets boundaries for your life: your time has boundaries—rest when it is time to rest; your finances have boundaries—give when you should give, save when you should save; your marriage has boundaries—never cross the line with anyone other than your spouse; your smartphone has boundaries—do not let the screen devour your solitary time with God.

**Boundaries are not bondage; boundaries are protection! Only with boundaries does the ocean become a magnificent sea; only with boundaries does life become a beautiful home!** [Emphasize]

God gave mankind free will, but He also established boundaries; only by honoring the boundaries can the blessings be preserved. For many people, the problem is not that God does not bless, but that when the blessing arrives, there are no boundaries to contain it—manna kept until morning breeds worms and rots, and blessings without boundaries will likewise spoil.

## (42:00 - 55:00) Part Three: Luminaries and Living Creatures—According to Their Kind, Ruling and Multiplying

### 3.1 The Mission of the Sun, Moon, and Stars: To "Rule" the Day and the Night

In verses 14-18, God created the sun, moon, and stars. Notice the verbs in verses 16 and 18: "the greater to **rule** the day, and the lesser to **rule** the night," and "to **rule** over the day and over the night."

The Hebrew word for "rule" is **Memshalah** (מֶמְשָׁלָה)—to manage, govern, have dominion.

Brothers and sisters, the sun, moon, and stars have three missions, stated very clearly in verses 14-15:

First, **to give light**—"let there be lights... to give light";  
Second, **to divide the day from the night, and let them be for signs and seasons, and for days and years**—to govern time;  
Third, **to rule over the day and over the night**—each keeping its proper place.

The sun never fights with the moon for a shift: the sun is on duty by day, the moon takes over by night, and the stars embellish the night sky. They do not strive, they do not grab, they do not overstep—**each according to its place, each fulfilling its duty!**

English comparison:
- KJV: "to **rule over** the day and over the night."
- NIV: "to **govern** the day and the night."

### 3.2 What Does "According to Its Kind" Mean in the Original Language?

In verses 11, 12, 21, 24, and 25, there is a phrase that appears repeatedly: **according to its kind**.

The Hebrew for "according to its kind" is **Lemino / Leminah**—according to its kind/species.

Herbs according to their kind, fruit trees according to their kind, great sea creatures according to their kind, winged birds according to their kind, beasts of the earth, cattle, and creeping things—**each according to its kind!**

Brothers and sisters, this is God's "identity manual" for all creation: **Live according to whatever kind you are; bear fruit within whatever kind you are.**

An apple tree does not envy a pear tree; a sparrow is not jealous of an eagle; a fish does not desire to fly in the sky; a bird does not desire to swim in the sea. Only when all creation is according to its kind is the world in good order.

What about human beings? The greatest misery of human beings is **refusing to be "according to their kind"**:

- Children do not want to be according to their kind—"other people's children" are great at everything, while my child falls short in every way;
- Husbands and wives do not want to be according to their kind—husbands envy other people's wives, wives envy other people's husbands;
- In the workplace, people do not want to be according to their kind—working in this industry while envying that one, drawing this salary while staring at that one;
- In the church, people do not want to be according to their kind—this one has gifts, that one has a platform, and I have nothing.

[Pause for 2 seconds]

Yet God says: **I created you with your own "kind," and your blessing is hidden within your "kind"!** An apple tree cannot bear pears, but an apple tree can bear the very best apples. You are not someone else; you are the one God meticulously created according to your "kind"—cultivate deeply within your "kind," and you will surely bear fruit!

### 3.3 "Be Fruitful and Multiply"—God's First Blessing

Verse 22: "And God blessed them, saying, '**Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let birds multiply on the earth.**'"

The Hebrew for "be fruitful and multiply" is **Peru Urevu**—be fruitful, multiply, reproduce, and increase.

Brothers and sisters, take note: this is **the very first blessing God spoke** in the Bible! The first recipient of God's spoken blessing was not man, but fish and birds! And the content of the blessing is: multiply! Fill! Double and increase!

Our God is a **God of multiplication**! He does not like "just enough"; He likes "overflowing abundantly": the seas filled with fish, the heavens filled with birds, and the earth teeming with herbs and trees. God's nature is abundance, multiplication, and fullness!

### 3.4 Golden Scripture Passages and Word Studies

**"The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament shows His handiwork. Day unto day utters speech, and night unto night reveals knowledge." (Ps. 19:1-2)**

Word Study: The Hebrew word for "declare" is **Saphar**, which means "to count, recount, relate, tell." The heavens are "recounting" God's glory every single day—sunrise and sunset, the alternation of day and night, that is the sky preaching!

Spoken Pulpit Exposition: The sun, moon, and stars cannot speak, yet they are "preaching" every day: the sun says, "God is faithful, rising every morning"; the stars say, "God is great, beyond all counting." **Every time you look up at the sky, you have listened to a sermon!**

---

**"Look at the birds of the air, for they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not of more value than they?" (Matt. 6:26)**

Spoken Pulpit Exposition: Jesus used the birds of Genesis chapter 1 to comfort anxious people. The birds of the air were created "according to their kind," and the heavenly Father "yet feeds them." **"Yet"—He feeds even the birds, how much more you?** You are created in the image of God; are you not of far more value than the birds?

---

**"Are not two sparrows sold for a copper coin? And not one of them falls to the ground apart from your Father’s will. But the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Do not fear therefore; you are of more value than many sparrows." (Matt. 10:29-31)**

Spoken Pulpit Exposition: Two sparrows for a copper coin—the cheapest living creatures at that time. Yet Jesus said: even such cheap sparrows are counted one by one by the heavenly Father, and "not one of them falls to the ground" apart from the Father's will. **If God cares even about the fall of a sparrow, will He not care about the falls in your life?** The very hairs of your head are all numbered—and every single tear you shed has been numbered by God as well!

---

**"Praise the Lord from the earth, you great sea creatures and all the depths; fire and hail, snow and clouds; stormy wind, fulfilling His word; mountains and all hills; fruitful trees and all cedars; beasts and all cattle; creeping things and flying fowl..." (Ps. 148:7-10, excerpt)**

Spoken Pulpit Exposition: In Psalm 148, the psalmist calls upon all creation by name to praise God: great sea creatures, hail, clouds, stormy wind, mountains, cedars, beasts, cattle, creeping things, flying fowl—**not a single one missing, a grand chorus of all!** All creation, each according to its kind, praises according to its kind. Brothers and sisters, all creation is praising; what about you?

### 3.5 Biblical Example: The Multiplication of the Five Loaves and Two Fish

In John chapter 6, five thousand people followed Jesus to hear Him preach. The day grew late, and everyone was hungry. The disciples said, "Master, send them away, for this is a deserted place."

Jesus said, "You give them something to eat."

The disciples were stunned: five thousand people! Philip did the math: "Two hundred denarii worth of bread is not sufficient for them, that every one of them may have a little."

At that moment, Andrew brought a boy forward: "There is a lad here who has **five barley loaves and two small fish**, but what are they among so many?"

Brothers and sisters, five loaves and two fish—a child's lunch. In human eyes, "what are they?" In God's eyes, **this is the seed of multiplication**!

Jesus took them, gave thanks, broke them, and distributed them to the crowd. Five thousand people ate their fill, and they gathered up **twelve baskets of fragments** left over!

[Pause for 2 seconds]

You see, God's mathematics is not like human mathematics:

Human math: 5 + 2 = 7, only enough for one child;  
God's math: 5 + 2 placed into Jesus' hands = 5,000 people fed + 12 baskets left over!

This is the **Peru Urevu** of Genesis 1:22—be fruitful, multiply, increase, and fill! God's first blessing is still in effect today: **Whatever little bit you have in your hand, as long as you place it into God's hands, God can make it multiply, increase, and fill "according to its kind"!**

Do you lack enough time? Surrender it, and God will multiply it.  
Do you lack enough money? Surrender it, and God will make it sufficient.  
Do you lack enough love? Surrender it, and God will enable you to love more.  
Is your gift very small? Surrender it, and God will make it bear fruit!

**In God's hands, being small is never the problem; refusing to surrender it is the problem!** [Emphasis]

Do not envy another person's "kind," nor despise your own "kind"; cultivate deeply within your own "kind" to the very end, and God's multiplication will naturally come. Many people dry up not because there is no blessing, but because they keep staring at someone else's "kind," leaving their own ground desolate.

## (55:00 - 60:00) Application for Six Groups of People · Weekly Exercise · Concluding Responsive Prayer

Speaker:  
Brothers and sisters, Genesis 1:4-25 is God's "construction log" of creation: separating, establishing, filling. Now, let us bring the lessons of these three passages into the lives of six groups of people:

**The First Group: Those who struggle with self-rejection and feel they are "not good enough."** Starting today, replace the evaluation system in your heart. The world says you are not good enough, but God says you are **Tov**—good! Very good! Every morning, look in the mirror and say: "God saw me, that it was good." This is not self-hypnosis; this is God's final verdict.

**The Second Group: Parents and teenagers.** According to its kind—your child has his own "kind," not the "kind" of "other people's children." An apple tree cannot bear pears; if you insist that it must bear pears, it will only wither away. Water it according to its "kind," fertilize it, wait patiently, and it will surely bear fruit. This week, say this to your child: "You are what God saw as good."

**Category Three: Married Couples.** Learn from God to "divide": separate the darkness out of your marriage—the silent treatment is darkness, separate it; secrets are darkness, separate them; ambiguous flirting on your phone is darkness, separate it. Let light be light, and darkness be darkness; only then can a marriage be something "God saw that it was good." Do one thing together this week as husband and wife: take one hidden matter and talk about it openly and honestly.

**Category Four: Workplace Professionals.** Learn from the sun, moon, and stars to "keep your appointed place": the sun does not take the moon's shift. When you shine, stay punctual, and act responsibly in your post, you are "ruling over" the "day and night" that God has entrusted to you. Do not spend every day eyeing someone else's position; first do your own "shift" well—"He who is faithful in what is least is faithful also in much" (Luke 16:10).

**Category Five: Entrepreneurs and Business People.** God's first blessing is **Peru Urevu**—multiplication! But remember the sequence: boundaries first (dry land), then produce (green herb). Establish boundaries in business first—boundaries of integrity, boundaries of cash flow, boundaries of family time—when boundaries are firmly set, multiplication will naturally follow. Place the "five loaves and two fish" in your hands into God's hands, and see what He will do.

**Category Six: The Elderly, the Sick, and Caregivers.** Listen to Jesus' words: "Look at the birds of the air... yet your heavenly Father feeds them." If God counts even the fall of a sparrow, how could He ever forget you? Your gray hairs, He has numbered; your tears, He has put into His bottle (Ps. 56:8). Old age is not "bad"; it is another season of "according to its kind"—the fruit of autumn is the sweetest.

💡 **Weekly Spiritual Practice**

**Practice One: The "God Saw That It Was Good" Declaration.** Every morning this week, stand in front of the mirror and say aloud three times: "God sees that I am good!" Then thank God for one specific thing—thank Him for a particular strength He created in you. After one week, you will find that the way you see yourself has changed.

**Practice Two: Establish a "Firmament Boundary."** Set a boundary for one thing in your life that is "overflowing": stay off your phone for 30 minutes before bed each night, being quiet before God; or set aside 15 minutes each day for Bible reading and prayer, rain or shine. Keep this boundary for one week, and see whether your "waters" turn into "provision."

### 🔥 Ignition Point Two: Today, Yes Today—Come Out of the Darkness!

Speaker:
[Slowing pace, scanning the congregation]

Brothers and sisters, Genesis 1:4 says that God "divided the light from the darkness."

Today, God is also going to make a "division" in your life—
To divide the light from your darkness;
To divide hope from your despair;
To divide freedom from your bondage;
To divide the "good" from the lie that "you are not good enough"!

[Raising volume, driving forward with parallel cadence]

Do not say tomorrow—
**Tomorrow, the darkness will still be there;**
**Today, the light has already come!**
**It is today, not tomorrow!**
**It is right now, not later!**

[Prophetic direct address]

In the name of the Lord, I declare to you:
That "darkness" in your life will be separated out today!
That knot in your heart will be untied today!
That burden you have carried for so long will be taken away today!

God said, "Let there be light"—
Today, God says to you: "**Come out of the darkness!**"

[Altar-call invitation, tone turning gentle yet firm]

Brothers and sisters, if you are willing today to walk out of the darkness and step into the light—

I invite you to make a response: place your hand over your heart, and pray along with me. You do not need to stand up; you do not need to come forward—God looks at the heart.

Let us bow our heads in prayer:

"Heavenly Father, we thank You! You are the Lord of creation; You look upon all that was made and say 'good,' and say 'very good.'

Lord, we confess: we often live in the evaluations of others, feeling that we are not good enough; we often lose our boundaries, allowing blessings to spoil; we often covet another's 'kind,' leaving our own field neglected.

Yet today, Your word comes to us: You say we are good, You set boundaries for us, You give each of us our own 'kind,' and You promise multiplication!

Lord, in Your name we declare: let light be divided from darkness! Let darkness leave our lives, and let light enter! From this day forward, we will live in Your evaluation, hold fast to Your boundaries, cultivate our 'kind' deeply, and receive Your multiplication!

Lord, we commit this coming week into Your hands. May Your *Tov* fill us, may Your boundaries protect us, and may Your *Peru Urevu* be fulfilled in our lives! In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ we pray, Amen!"
