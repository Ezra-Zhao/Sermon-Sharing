## Content

**What changes does being saved bring?** This is a fact, not a theory. Salvation will surely produce its effect in us and cause those who receive grace to have a marked change — not merely manifested in knowledge and words, but flowing out in life and living. What changes, exactly, does a person who is saved have?

**Change of life.** *Christ formed in us:* "…until Christ is formed in you." *Christ as life:* "Christ… is our life." *Christ magnified:* "…that… Christ will be magnified in my body, whether by life or by death." A person who is saved has received Christ to be formed within him; God has given him a new life, making him live no longer as himself, but Christ living in him. Not only living within, He can also be manifested outwardly.

**Change of nature.** *Partakers of the divine nature:* "…having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust, [we] may be partakers of the divine nature." *Loving righteousness:* "You have loved righteousness…" (1 Cor. — as cited in the original). *Hating wickedness:* "…and hated wickedness; therefore God, Your God, [has anointed You]." When life has changed, nature will surely also change, because every kind of life has its own characteristics. A person who partakes of God's life will surely partake of God's nature. Originally he loved sin; now he hates sin! ("…in the righteousness and true holiness [of the truth].")

**Change of living.** *Fellowship with God:* "…truly our fellowship is with the Father and with His Son Jesus Christ." *Relying on God's grace:* "…not with fleshly wisdom but in the grace of God." *Doing God's will:* "…that you may stand perfect… having done the will of God" (in the original: "that you… may do the will of God"). The most marked effect of salvation in us is that it has changed our relationship with God. When we partake of God's life and nature, we naturally long for fellowship with God and walk in God's will.

**Change of thinking.** *A change in gain and loss:* "…what things were gain to me, these I have counted loss…" *A change between heaven and earth:* "Set your mind on things above, not on things on the earth." *A change between spirit and flesh:* "For to be carnally minded is death, but to be spiritually minded is life and peace." Thinking is the rudder of life: as a man thinks, so he becomes. For this reason, in salvation the Lord takes back all man's thoughts and makes them captive to obey Christ. Thus, in salvation we have a great change in our thinking: we no longer set our mind on earthly things but on heavenly things; we no longer take ourselves as the center but take God as the center.

### Homework Questions

1. What change is there in the life of one who has been saved?
2. Has Christ been formed in you?
3. What change is there in the nature of one who has been saved?
4. After being saved, have you had the experience of hating sin yet sinning?
5. What change is there in the living of one who has been saved?
6. Does every saved Christian have such a change in living?
7. What change is there in the thinking of one who has been saved?
