## Content

By what can we be completely saved? We know that the foundation of salvation is the Son of God, our Lord Jesus Christ. We also know that the completion of salvation lies in trusting in Christ's precious blood, looking to His cross, and waiting for His second coming. These three things constitute the complete salvation.

The precious blood of Christ ("...knowing that you were not redeemed with corruptible things... but with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot" (1 Pet. 1:18-19, NKJV)). 1. That sins may be forgiven: "For this is My blood of the new covenant, which is shed for many for the remission of sins" (Matt. 26:28, NKJV). 2. Obtaining redemption: "In Him we have redemption through His blood" (Eph. 1:7; Col. 1:14, NKJV). 3. Drawing near to God: "...by the blood of Jesus [you] have been brought near" (Eph. 2:13, NKJV). 4. Inheriting eternal life: "Whoever eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life" (John 6:54, NKJV). Because of Christ's precious blood, the foundation of the new covenant was made (Hebrews). Not only are our sins forgiven, and we turn from Satan's authority to God (Acts); not only are we reconciled to God, but we also have God's life. A person who has a share in Christ's precious blood — his sins have already been covered. [Original text partially illegible.] When wrath and judgment come, he will be passed over because of the mark of the blood (1 Thessalonians), because the Lord's blood will plead for him, and "the blood of sprinkling... speaks better things than that of Abel" (Heb. 12:24, NKJV).

The cross of Christ ("I have been crucified with Christ; it is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me; and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself for me" (Gal. 2:20, NKJV)). 1. The body of sin done away: "knowing this, that our old man was crucified with Him, that the body of sin might be done away with" (Rom. 6:6, NKJV). 2. The world crucified: "But God forbid that I should boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world" (Gal. 6:14, NKJV). 3. The enemy taken captive: "Having disarmed principalities and powers, He made a public spectacle of them, triumphing over them in it [the cross]" (Col. 2:15, NKJV). Christ's precious blood causes us to receive grace; Christ's cross causes us to be victorious. The precious blood refers to Christ dying in our place; the cross refers to our dying with Christ. We not only, in the negative aspect, escape judgment by the precious blood; we even more need, in the positive aspect, to overcome the enemy by the cross. Our enemies are three: first, the flesh; second, the world; third, Satan. All these enemies need to be overcome by relying on the cross.

The return of Christ. 1. Saving the believers: "to those who eagerly wait for Him He will appear a second time, apart from sin, for salvation" (Heb. 9:28, NKJV). 2. The redemption of the body: "eagerly waiting for the adoption, the redemption of our body" (Rom. 8:23, NKJV). 3. Being like the Lord: "when He is revealed, we shall be like Him" (1 John 3:2, NKJV) (1 Corinthians). The final completion of salvation still awaits Christ's return. If He does not return for one day, the believers who have fallen asleep must sleep one more day; if He does not return for three years, the living believers must wait for Him three more years. All who have received grace today are eagerly longing for the Lord, as the deer pants for the water brooks. They pray without ceasing, saying: "Lord Jesus, I wish You would come!"

Homework questions: [Original text partially illegible.] On which three things must we rely? What is the efficacy of the precious blood? Try to give four points. What is the difference between what Christ's blood speaks and what Abel's blood speaks? What is the difference between the function of the cross and the function of the precious blood? How many enemies do we have? Is Christ's saving at His return related to sin? Do you long for Christ's salvation at His return?
